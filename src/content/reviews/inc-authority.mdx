## What Is Inc Authority?

Inc Authority is a business formation service founded in 1989 and headquartered in Reno, Nevada. They've operated for over 35 years, formed hundreds of thousands of business entities, and built one of the longest track records in the industry.

Their business model is the inverse of most competitors. Most formation services lead with a low headline price and monetize through subscriptions and renewals. Inc Authority leads with $0 — no service charge, ever — and monetizes almost entirely through optional upsells during checkout and the first year (registered agent renewal, EIN filing, operating agreement, S-corp election, business funding analysis, premium document storage, and so on).

If you walk through their flow, decline every upsell, and pay only your state filing fee, you genuinely get an LLC and a year of registered agent service for $0 in service charges. That's not marketing — it's the actual product.

## Inc Authority LLC Pricing

### Starter (Free) — $0 + State Filing Fee

The free tier covers:

- Business name availability check in your state
- LLC formation document prep & filing
- Free registered agent service for year one
- Tax planning consultation
- Business credit and funding analysis
- Digital document delivery and storage

This is the headline offer. Every item above is included at no service charge.

### Premium Tiers — Pricing Revealed at Checkout

Inc Authority offers paid premium bundles (Starter Business Bundle, Fast Track, and similar) that include items like the operating agreement, EIN filing, S-corp tax election, expedited filing, business funding analysis, and premium support. **Their pricing is not published on the public site** — it's revealed during the signup flow, and bundle composition can change.

This is unusual in the industry, and it's the single biggest practical drawback. With ZenBusiness or Northwest, you can compare plan prices on the website before signing up. With Inc Authority, you're getting the price during a sales-driven checkout flow. If transparent upfront pricing matters to you, this will frustrate you.

## What's Included (and What's Not)

**Included in the free Starter tier:**
- LLC formation — Articles of Organization filing
- Registered agent service for year one
- Tax planning consultation (typically a phone call with one of their advisors)
- Business credit and funding analysis
- Digital document delivery

**Not included at the free tier (paid upsells):**
- **Operating agreement** — sold as a paid upsell. Most competitors include at least a template even on free tiers.
- **EIN filing** — paid upsell. You can file this yourself for free at the [IRS website](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online).
- **BOI report filing** — paid upsell.
- **Compliance alerts and annual report filing** — paid upsell.

**Not included at any tier:**
- **Privacy protection** — Inc Authority does not use their own address on public filings. [Northwest Registered Agent](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) is the only top service that solves this in the base package.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Inc Authority legit?

Yes. Inc Authority has been operating since 1989 — one of the longest track records in the business formation industry. They hold a 4.9-star Trustpilot rating across more than 46,000 reviews and have filed hundreds of thousands of business entities.

### Is the Inc Authority free LLC really free?

Yes. The Starter tier files your Articles of Organization at no service charge — you pay only your state's filing fee. Registered agent service is also free for the first year. The free offer is real. Operating agreement, EIN filing, and BOI reporting are paid upsells.

### Does Inc Authority charge for registered agent service?

It's free for the first year. After year one, registered agent service renews at a paid rate (typically $199+/year). You can transfer to a separate registered agent provider before renewal if you want to avoid the renewal charge.

### How does Inc Authority make money on $0 LLCs?

Through paid upsells during checkout (operating agreement, EIN filing, S-corp election, premium bundles) and through registered agent renewals after year one. The model is similar to other "freemium" formation services, but Inc Authority leans on it harder than most.

### How long does Inc Authority take to form an LLC?

Standard processing is 10–15 business days. Faster filing is available through paid premium bundles. State approval times vary independently.

### Does Inc Authority offer a money-back guarantee?

Inc Authority's refund policy is more limited than top competitors. The free Starter plan has no service charge to refund (you pay only state fees, which the state doesn't refund). For paid upsells, read current refund terms carefully before purchase.
