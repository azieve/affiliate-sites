## What Is Tailor Brands?

Tailor Brands was founded in 2014 in New York and built its early reputation as one of the first AI-powered logo design platforms. In 2022 they layered LLC formation on top of that branding stack, positioning themselves as the place to launch both a legal business entity and a complete brand identity in one workflow.

That's a meaningfully different pitch from a pure formation service. ZenBusiness, Northwest, and LegalZoom are formation-first companies that may sell you tangentially related extras. Tailor Brands is a branding-first company that added formation to keep customers inside their ecosystem longer.

## Tailor Brands LLC Pricing Plans

Tailor Brands sells LLC formation across three tiers. The pricing below is straightforward, but read carefully — every plan above Lite is an annual subscription that auto-renews.

### Lite — $0 + State Filing Fee

The free entry tier covers:

- LLC formation, filed in 14 business days
- Operating agreement
- Annual compliance filing & updates
- Business coaching platform access
- Basic branding tools — 1-year domain, 8 logos, simple website builder

It is genuinely $0 from Tailor Brands. You pay only your state's filing fee. The catch is the 14-day standard processing — slow compared to [Northwest's same-day filing](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) or Bizee's 3-5 business days.

### Essential — $199/year + State Filing Fee

The Essential plan is where most paying customers land:

- Everything in Lite
- 1 business day expedited LLC filing
- 30-day invoicing & bookkeeping trial
- Business coaching program
- $30 Amazon gift card bonus

The expedited filing alone is the main reason to upgrade. Going from 14 days to 1 business day is a meaningful difference if you need to open a bank account or sign contracts quickly.

### Elite — $249/year + State Filing Fee

The top tier adds:

- Everything in Essential
- Domain name included
- Website hosting included
- $50 Amazon gift card bonus

Elite is positioned for customers who would have bought a domain and hosting separately anyway. If you're not building a website, the upgrade isn't worth it.

### What's Not Bundled

Two things you'd expect to be included aren't:

- **Registered agent service** — sold as a paid add-on, not bundled into any LLC plan.
- **EIN filing** — also a paid add-on. You can do this yourself for free at the [IRS website](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) in about 10 minutes.

Both are relevant ongoing costs to factor in.

## What's Included (and What's Not)

**Included on every plan:**
- LLC Formation — Articles of Organization filing in your state of choice
- Operating agreement
- Annual compliance filings & updates (state annual reports)
- AI logo maker, basic website builder, business coaching platform

**Not included at any tier:**
- Registered agent service (paid add-on)
- EIN filing (paid add-on)
- BOI report filing (paid add-on)
- Privacy protection on public filings — your provided address goes on the public record. [Northwest Registered Agent](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) is the only top service that includes privacy protection in its base package.

## Tailor Brands Pros and Cons

### What We Like

**1. The Lite plan is a real $0 LLC.** No tricks — you pay only state fees. Slow processing (14 days), but no service charge.

**2. AI-powered branding tools are bundled in.** Logo maker, basic website builder, and business coaching come with every plan. If you'd build a brand identity anyway, the marginal cost of formation through Tailor Brands is essentially zero.

**3. Same-day expedited filing on Essential and Elite.** Faster than ZenBusiness's 4-6 day expedited processing.

**4. Operating agreement on every plan.** Even the free tier includes a usable operating agreement template — not every competitor does that.

**5. Annual compliance filings included.** State annual reports are filed for you, which removes a real source of ongoing administrative burden.

### What We Don't Like

**1. Mixed Trustpilot reviews.** The Tailor Brands Trustpilot footprint is smaller and lower-rated than top formation-focused competitors. Recurring complaints center on auto-renewing subscriptions and difficulty cancelling.

**2. Auto-renewing yearly subscriptions.** Essential and Elite both auto-renew at full price. If you only needed the expedited filing, set a calendar reminder before renewal.

**3. Registered agent and EIN are paid add-ons.** Both [Northwest](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) and [Bizee](/reviews/bizee/) include free first-year registered agent service in their entry tiers.

**4. No privacy protection.** Your address appears on public filings. Northwest is the only top service that solves this in the base package.

**5. Branding-first identity.** If you're past the launch phase or already have branding handled, you'd be paying for tools you won't use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tailor Brands legit?

Yes. Tailor Brands has been operating since 2014 and added LLC formation to its product line in 2022. The company is headquartered in New York and serves customers across all 50 states.

### Is the Tailor Brands free LLC really free?

The Lite plan files your Articles of Organization at no service charge — you pay only your state's filing fee. Standard processing is 14 business days. There is no hidden charge for the formation itself, but registered agent service and EIN filing are paid add-ons if you need them.

### Does Tailor Brands include a registered agent?

No. Registered agent service is sold separately as a paid add-on. Every LLC legally needs a registered agent, so factor this into your total cost.

### How long does Tailor Brands take to form an LLC?

The Lite plan files within 14 business days. The Essential and Elite plans expedite filing to 1 business day. State approval times vary independently and are outside Tailor Brands' control.

### Does Tailor Brands offer a money-back guarantee?

Tailor Brands' refund policy is more limited than competitors like ZenBusiness or Northwest. Read the current terms carefully before subscribing — particularly around the annual auto-renew on Essential and Elite plans.

## Our Bottom Line

Tailor Brands is genuinely useful for one specific buyer: someone launching both an LLC and a brand at the same time, who doesn't already have a logo or website. The Lite plan is a legitimate $0 LLC, the Essential plan files in one business day, and the bundled branding tools work. The combination is rare in this market.

For everyone else — anyone who just needs an LLC filed without branding extras — [ZenBusiness](/reviews/zenbusiness/) is a better starting point. Cleaner experience, far better customer reviews, and a more polished compliance feature set on the paid tier.
