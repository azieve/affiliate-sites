## Who Should Choose LLC Attorney?

**LLC Attorney is the right call when:**

**Your ownership structure is non-standard.** If you're forming a multi-member LLC with complex profit-sharing arrangements, a manager-managed structure with separate voting rights, or provisions around intellectual property contribution, having an attorney review the operating agreement is worth every dollar. Template agreements don't account for nuance, and ambiguities in operating agreements are where the most expensive disputes originate.

## What the IRS Actually Cares About

Here's a nuance most comparison articles skip entirely: the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc) does not evaluate the drafting quality of your Articles of Organization. Your LLC's tax classification — disregarded entity for single-member, partnership for multi-member — is determined by federal law and your own elections, not by the language in your state filing documents.

Where legal accuracy genuinely matters is in your operating agreement, specifically around:

- How profits and losses are allocated among members
- Member voting rights and decision-making authority
- What happens when a member wants to exit the company
- How new members can be admitted and at what ownership percentage
- How the LLC is dissolved if it comes to that

If you're using a ZenBusiness template operating agreement and your business is a standard single-owner or simple two-owner structure, the template is generally sufficient. If you're deviating from standard terms in any meaningful way, that's precisely when attorney review justifies its premium.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does it cost to form an LLC with LLC Attorney vs ZenBusiness?**

**Do I legally need an attorney to form an LLC?**

No. An attorney is not legally required to form an LLC in any U.S. state. Formation services like ZenBusiness handle the entire filing process — Articles of Organization, registered agent appointment, operating agreement, and EIN — without attorney involvement. Attorney review can add value for complex ownership structures or regulated industries, but it is not a legal requirement.

**What happens if my ZenBusiness filing is rejected by the state?**

ZenBusiness will correct and refile at no additional charge. State rejections most commonly result from business name conflicts or missing information in the filing. ZenBusiness's support team handles these situations routinely and will communicate with you about what needs to be corrected.

**Is LLC Attorney a legitimate service?**

Yes. LLC Attorney is a legitimate formation service backed by licensed attorneys who review formation documents before state submission. As with any service, you should carefully review what specific attorney services are included in your chosen package — and confirm whether ongoing legal advice is available or if the review is limited to the formation documents themselves.

**Does ZenBusiness include registered agent service?**

Yes. All ZenBusiness plans include registered agent service. On the Starter plan, the first year is bundled with formation at no additional charge. After the first year, registered agent renewal runs $199/year. Unlike some competitors that obscure the registered agent cost until checkout, ZenBusiness is upfront about this structure.

**Can I switch from LLC Attorney to ZenBusiness after my LLC is formed?**

Yes. Your registered agent can be changed at any time by filing a Statement of Change of Registered Agent with your state — fees are typically $10–$50 depending on the state. The LLC itself remains unchanged; you're simply changing the service provider for ongoing registered agent and compliance support.

**Which service is faster for forming an LLC?**

Both services offer standard and expedited filing options. ZenBusiness's standard processing is typically 3–7 business days after the state reviews the filing — though state processing times vary significantly and are largely outside any formation service's control. LLC Attorney timelines are comparable. Both offer rush filing upgrades for faster turnaround.

**When does the operating agreement actually matter?**

Your operating agreement matters most when there's a dispute — between co-founders over profit distributions, when adding or removing a member, or when dissolving the company. For single-member LLCs, the operating agreement is primarily important for maintaining your liability protection (it establishes that your LLC is a separate legal entity) and for some banking requirements. Multi-member LLCs have significantly higher stakes around operating agreement quality, which is where attorney review adds the most value.
