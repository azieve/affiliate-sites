## What Each Company Actually Does

### LegalZoom in 2026

[LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) is the household name in online legal services. It sells LLC formation in tiered packages. The entry tier has a low service fee (state filing fees are always extra), and higher tiers bundle things like an operating agreement, EIN assistance and expedited filing. Its registered agent service is offered separately and renewed annually.

LegalZoom also sells trademarks, wills, contracts and attorney consultations, which is useful if you want everything under one login. Our full [LegalZoom review](/blog/legalzoom-review-2026/) covers every tier in detail.

### Registered Agents Inc

Registered Agents Inc is a Wyoming-based company that built its reputation on being a registered agent, and it markets heavily around privacy. It will form your LLC for a modest service fee plus your state's filing fee, and then act as your registered agent. We cover this in more depth in our [Registered Agents Inc review](/blog/registered-agents-inc-review/).

The tradeoff is breadth. You are not getting a polished dashboard full of compliance reminders and business tools. You are getting a focused service that does a few things well.

## Pricing Compared: What You'll Really Pay

Pricing is where most people make the wrong call, because the sticker price on day one is not the cost over three years.

### LegalZoom pricing

LegalZoom's formation packages have typically run on this pattern:

- **Basic:** a low or $0 service fee, with state filing fees extra. Basic generally leaves out the operating agreement and EIN help, and expedited processing costs more.
- **Standard:** around $249, adding items such as an operating agreement and a registered agent for the first year.
- **Premium:** around $299, adding further extras and faster handling.

The **LegalZoom registered agent cost** after the included period is the one that surprises people. At roughly $299 per year in many cases, it is about three times what a dedicated agent charges. This is the biggest hidden cost I flag for readers.

### Registered Agents Inc pricing

Registered Agents Inc's formation service fee is typically far lower, in the neighborhood of $50 plus state fees, and its annual registered agent fee has been around $100. There are usually no tiers to decode, which makes budgeting easy.

## Registered Agent Service: Privacy, Reliability and Mail Handling

A registered agent receives lawsuits and official state notices on your LLC's behalf during business hours. Missing one can mean a default judgment, so this is not a place to cut corners. If you want the basics first, read [what a registered agent is and whether you need one](/blog/what-is-a-registered-agent-and-do-i-need-one/).

### Privacy

This is Registered Agents Inc's strongest argument. The company has long positioned itself around keeping owners' personal information out of public records, for example by using its own address on filings. LegalZoom also lets you use its agent address, but privacy is not the core of its pitch.

If keeping your name and home address out of public view is your priority, read our guide on [forming an anonymous LLC](/blog/anonymous-llc-how-to-form-one/) first. Privacy depends heavily on your state: some states publish member or manager names, while others, like New Mexico, are far more private.

For anyone who ranks privacy above all else, [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) is the other name that belongs in the conversation. Its model includes its address on your filings as standard, and we rate it highly for privacy in our [Northwest review](/blog/northwest-registered-agent-review-2026/).

### Document handling

In my experience, the real test of a registered agent is not the price. It is what happens on the day a process server shows up. I've seen owners discover a lawsuit weeks late because their agent only sent a generic email with no scan of the document. Before buying from anyone, check how they notify you (email, text, portal), how fast they scan and forward, and whether the scan is included or a fee.

## Formation Features Compared

### Operating agreement and EIN

An operating agreement is not required to file in every state, but you should have one, especially with multiple members. LegalZoom generally includes one only in higher tiers. Registered Agents Inc has historically been lighter here. Our [operating agreement guide](/blog/llc-operating-agreement-template-and-guide/) explains what to include and why.

You can also get your EIN directly from the IRS at no cost. Any provider charging you for an EIN is charging for convenience. See how to [get an EIN for free using the IRS method](/blog/how-to-get-an-ein-for-your-llc-free-irs-method/), and note the IRS's own page on [applying for an Employer Identification Number](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) is the authoritative source.

### Compliance reminders and ongoing support

Formation is a one-time event. Annual reports, franchise taxes and renewals are forever. Missing them can put your LLC out of good standing, which can cost you liability protection in practice and fees to reinstate. See [what happens if you don't renew your LLC](/blog/what-happens-if-you-don-t-renew-your-llc/).

LegalZoom offers more in the way of reminders and add-on compliance products. Registered Agents Inc is leaner. If you will not track deadlines yourself, weigh that gap seriously.

### Customer support

LegalZoom has a large support operation and optional attorney consultations at extra cost. Registered Agents Inc is known for answering the phone with real staff, which owners often value more than a polished chat widget. Customer experience varies, so read recent reviews before committing.

## Tax and Legal Considerations Before You Pick

Your formation provider does not decide your taxes. By default, a single-member LLC is taxed as a sole proprietorship and a multi-member LLC as a partnership, and you can elect S-corporation treatment later if it makes sense. That is a decision to make with a CPA, not a checkbox on a formation website. Our guides on [how LLCs are taxed](/blog/llc-taxes-how-are-llcs-taxed/) and [LLC vs S-corp](/blog/llc-vs-s-corp-which-is-better-for-taxes/) explain the tradeoffs.

Also keep in mind that the Small Business Administration offers general guidance on [choosing a business structure](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure), which is a useful neutral cross-check on anything a commercial provider tells you.

On beneficial ownership reporting, federal rules have shifted repeatedly in recent years, and domestic companies' obligations have changed substantially. Check current FinCEN guidance or see our [BOI report changes and updates for 2026](/blog/boi-report-changes-and-updates-2026/) before assuming you do or do not need to file.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Registered Agents Inc better than LegalZoom?

It depends on your priorities. Registered Agents Inc typically has lower registered agent pricing and a stronger privacy focus. LegalZoom offers a bigger product catalog and brand recognition. On pure cost over several years, the agent-focused provider is usually cheaper.

### How much does the LegalZoom registered agent cost?

LegalZoom's registered agent service has generally been priced around $299 per year after any included first-year period, which is notably higher than many competitors. Confirm the current rate on its site before buying.

### How much does Registered Agents Inc charge for an LLC?

Its formation service fee has typically been around $50 plus your state's filing fee, with registered agent service around $100 per year. Pricing can change, so verify before you order.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in that state. You can act as your own agent, but then your name and address typically become public and you must be available during business hours. See our [registered agent guide](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/).

### Can I switch registered agents after forming my LLC?

Yes. You file a change-of-agent form with your state, usually for a small fee, and your new agent accepts the appointment. Many owners use this to escape high renewal rates.

### Is it cheaper to form an LLC myself?

Filing directly with your state avoids service fees, and you pay only the state filing fee. The tradeoff is time and the chance of paperwork errors. Our [LLC cost breakdown](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-form-an-llc/) shows the state-by-state range.

### Which is best for privacy, LegalZoom or Registered Agents Inc?

Registered Agents Inc generally leans harder into privacy. Still, real anonymity depends on your state's disclosure rules, so read your state's requirements before relying on any provider's claims.
