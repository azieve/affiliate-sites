## What Is BetterLegal?

BetterLegal is an online LLC and corporation formation service founded in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. Unlike most LLC formation services — which offer tiered plans ranging from a free basic option to a premium package — BetterLegal operates on a flat-fee model. One package, one price.

The company's core value proposition is speed. They process filings through a largely automated backend, which reduces human handling time on their end. In their marketing, BetterLegal claims same-day or 24-hour turnaround in many states — a claim that's partially true, with an important caveat: your actual approval timeline is determined by your state's Secretary of State office, not by BetterLegal's processing speed. In states like Texas, Delaware, and Wyoming — where SOS offices already process filings in 24–72 hours — BetterLegal's speed advantage over competitors narrows significantly.

## BetterLegal Pricing: The Flat-Fee Model Explained

BetterLegal's pricing structure is simple by design. There is one package: **$299 in service fees plus your state's filing fee**.

State filing fees vary considerably — from around $35 in states like Kentucky and New Mexico to over $500 in Massachusetts. Most states fall in the $50–$150 range. So your total BetterLegal LLC formation cost typically runs **$350–$450** depending on where you're filing.

Here's what BetterLegal pricing includes in that $299:

- Filing your Articles of Organization with the state
- Registered agent service for the first year
- EIN (Employer Identification Number) application with the IRS
- Digital document delivery

After year one, registered agent service renews at **$99/year** — a reasonable rate compared to the industry average of $100–$150/year.

Where BetterLegal pricing starts to look expensive is in direct comparison. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) offers LLC formation starting at **$0 + state fees** on their Starter plan, which includes registered agent service for the first year and EIN filing — the same core services BetterLegal charges $299 for. ZenBusiness's Pro plan at **$199/year** and Premium at **$349/year** layer in compliance alerts, an operating agreement template, and ongoing support tools.

[LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) follows a similar structure, with free base-tier formation and premium packages running $249–$299/year that include legal consultations and attorney access.

Paying $299 for BetterLegal's service when capable competitors start at zero is a hard case to make unless speed is a mission-critical requirement — and even then, the speed gap is often overstated.

## What's Included — Features Broken Down

Let's be specific about what BetterLegal's $299 package covers, and equally important, what it doesn't.

**What you get:**

- **Articles of Organization filing** — BetterLegal prepares and submits your LLC formation documents to the state. This is the core of any formation service.
- **Registered agent service (Year 1)** — They serve as your [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) for 12 months, receiving legal correspondence and official notices on your behalf. After that, $99/year.
- **EIN application** — BetterLegal files for your federal Employer Identification Number. Most LLCs need an EIN to open a business bank account, hire employees, or elect S-corp tax treatment.
- **Clean digital interface** — The user experience is genuinely well-designed. The onboarding process is simple, and documents are delivered cleanly.

**What's missing:**

- **Operating agreement** — BetterLegal does not include an operating agreement. This is the most significant gap in their package. An operating agreement is your LLC's internal governance document — it specifies ownership percentages, how profits and losses are divided, management authority, and what happens when a member wants to exit. Many states require it; banks and investors often ask for it; and without one, disputes between members default to state default rules, which rarely reflect what founders actually intended. ZenBusiness, LegalZoom, [Tailor Brands](/go/tailor-brands/), and [Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/) all include operating agreement templates. BetterLegal does not.
- **Annual report reminders** — Missing an annual report filing can result in your LLC being administratively dissolved. BetterLegal provides no compliance calendar or deadline alerts.
- **Ongoing compliance dashboard** — No client portal for tracking your LLC's compliance status, filing history, or upcoming deadlines.
- **Business banking assistance** — No help with opening a business bank account, which ZenBusiness includes at higher tiers.
- **Amended filings** — Changes to your LLC (new members, address updates, name changes) incur additional fees.

In my experience advising business owners, the operating agreement gap is the issue that creates real problems down the road. I've seen too many single-member LLCs treated as sole proprietorships in a dispute because there was no formal governance document, and too many multi-member LLCs tear apart over questions that a two-page operating agreement would have answered at formation. BetterLegal's omission of this document isn't just a minor missing feature — it's a foundational gap.

## BetterLegal Complaints and Red Flags to Know

Any thorough BetterLegal review has to address what real customers report. The overall picture is mixed.

**What customers praise:** The onboarding process is genuinely fast and clean. For founders who've struggled through clunky competitor interfaces, BetterLegal's minimalist flow is a relief. Speed claims hold up in fast-processing states.

**Recurring complaints:**

**Post-formation upsells.** A pattern that emerges in any BetterLegal review aggregation — Trustpilot, Google Reviews, Reddit — is frustration with add-on offers after formation. The "flat fee" simplicity of the initial pitch gives way to a familiar upsell sequence once your LLC is filed. This is industry-wide behavior, but it undercuts BetterLegal's stated differentiation.

**Customer service response times.** Multiple reviews report difficulty reaching support when issues arise — wrong state filings, missing documents, or questions about ongoing compliance. For a service priced at $299, premium support responsiveness should be expected.

**No operating agreement.** This crops up repeatedly in negative reviews: founders who assumed a $299 complete-package formation service would include a governance document, and discovered post-filing that it didn't.

**Limited ongoing value.** BetterLegal is a transactional service. Once your LLC is formed, the relationship largely ends. There's no ongoing compliance dashboard, no account manager, no renewal reminders. Services like ZenBusiness are designed to grow with your business; BetterLegal is designed to file one document.

According to data from the [U.S. Small Business Administration](https://www.sba.gov/), new business formation in the U.S. has remained elevated through 2025 and into 2026, with millions of new entities filed annually. That competitive environment has driven top-tier formation services to increase feature depth and reduce prices — making BetterLegal's static pricing model increasingly difficult to defend.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use BetterLegal

**BetterLegal may make sense if:**

- You're in a state where BetterLegal's automated processing genuinely reduces time-to-filing — particularly states with slower standard SOS processing.
- You already have your own attorney-drafted operating agreement and don't need one included.
- You're forming a single-member LLC with simple compliance needs and you simply prefer a no-decision purchase experience.
- You've calculated that the time savings justify the $299 premium over a free-tier competitor.

**BetterLegal is likely the wrong choice if:**

- You're forming a multi-member LLC where an operating agreement is functionally non-negotiable.
- You're cost-conscious — ZenBusiness handles the same core filing tasks for $0 + state fees.
- You want ongoing compliance support, deadline reminders, or a document dashboard.
- You're in a state like Texas, Delaware, Wyoming, or Florida where same-day or 24-hour filing is already standard at the SOS level, regardless of which service you use.
- You're a new business owner who wants a formation partner, not just a one-time filing service.

## Frequently Asked Questions About BetterLegal

**How much does BetterLegal charge for LLC formation?**

BetterLegal charges a flat $299 service fee plus your state's filing fee. State fees range from roughly $35 to over $500 depending on your state. Registered agent service renews at $99/year after the first year, which is included in the initial $299. Total first-year costs typically fall between $350–$450 for most states.

**Does BetterLegal include an operating agreement?**

No. BetterLegal does not include an operating agreement in their standard package. This is a meaningful omission — an operating agreement establishes how your LLC is governed, how profits are split, and what happens when members exit. Most competing services, including ZenBusiness, LegalZoom, and Northwest, include operating agreement templates at no additional cost.

**How fast does BetterLegal actually process filings?**

BetterLegal's automated processing prepares and submits your documents quickly — typically same-day on their end. Your actual LLC approval date depends on your state's Secretary of State office processing times, which vary from same-day (Delaware, Wyoming) to several weeks (California, New York). BetterLegal cannot override state processing timelines.

**Is BetterLegal a legitimate company?**

Yes. BetterLegal is a legitimate LLC formation service incorporated in Texas and operating since 2018. They have real customer reviews on Trustpilot and Google, mostly positive. Legitimacy and best value are separate questions — BetterLegal is a real company, but most business owners will get more for less money with ZenBusiness or LegalZoom in 2026.

**Can BetterLegal serve as my registered agent long-term?**

Yes. After the first year included in the $299 package, BetterLegal offers ongoing registered agent service for $99/year. This is competitive with industry norms. For background on why every LLC is required to maintain a registered agent, see our guide on [what is a registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/).

**What states does BetterLegal operate in?**

BetterLegal offers BetterLegal LLC formation in all 50 states. Their speed advantage is most meaningful in states with slower standard SOS processing, where their automated submission pipeline can reduce handling time versus a manual submission workflow.

**Does BetterLegal help with S-corp elections?**

BetterLegal's standard package focuses on LLC formation. An S-corp election (IRS Form 2553) is a separate step not included in their base offering. According to [IRS guidance on S-corporations](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporations), an S-corp election is often worth pursuing once your LLC's net profit consistently exceeds $50,000–$80,000 annually. Consult a tax professional to determine timing and handle the election correctly.

**Is BetterLegal worth it compared to free alternatives?**

For most business owners in 2026: no. Services like ZenBusiness offer the same core formation filing for $0 + state fees, plus features BetterLegal omits at any price — including an operating agreement and compliance tracking. BetterLegal's $299 fee is justifiable only for founders who place significant monetary value on their specific processing workflow and don't need an operating agreement or ongoing compliance support.
