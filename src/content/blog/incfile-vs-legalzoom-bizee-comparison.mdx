## What Happened to Incfile? The Bizee Rebrand Explained

Incfile was one of the original disruptors in the LLC formation space — it introduced the now-common "$0 + state fees" model that forced the entire industry to rethink pricing. In 2023, the company officially rebranded as Bizee, citing a desire to better reflect its broader suite of business services beyond just formation.

The rebrand was largely cosmetic. The website changed, the logo changed, and new branding rolled out across social channels — but the underlying formation platform, pricing structure, and service tiers remained substantially similar. If you formed an LLC with Incfile in 2019, your dashboard is now called Bizee. Your registered agent service transferred over. Your documents are the same.

What the rebrand did signal, though, is an intent to compete more directly with [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) and [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) in the post-formation services category: registered agent, annual report filing, EIN procurement, operating agreements, and small business legal documents. ## Features Compared: What You Actually Get

Price alone doesn't tell the whole story. Here's how the two services compare on the features that matter most to new business owners in 2026.

### Registered Agent Service

Both services include one free year of registered agent service with any paid or free formation plan. This is the person or company authorized to receive legal documents on behalf of your LLC — a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) is required in every state.

After year one:
- **Bizee:** $119/year
- **LegalZoom:** $249/year

If budget is your primary concern, Bizee is the clear winner here. Worth noting: [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) offers registered agent service at $125/year with a notably stronger privacy-forward approach, and its formation service — while not free — is widely regarded as the most full-featured in the market. See our full [Northwest Registered Agent review](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) for details.

### EIN (Employer Identification Number)

Bizee includes EIN procurement in its Gold plan ($199). LegalZoom includes it in its Pro plan ($249). Both services handle the IRS filing on your behalf, though you can also [get an EIN directly from the IRS for free](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) if you're comfortable with the online application.

### Operating Agreement

Both services include an operating agreement template in their mid-tier plans. Bizee's is a standard single-member or multi-member template; LegalZoom's is similar but with slightly more customization prompts. Neither replaces the work of an actual attorney for complex ownership structures — but for a solo LLC or a two-person partnership, either is adequate.

### Legal Access and Attorney Consultations

This is where LegalZoom has a genuine, durable advantage. Its Premium plan and subscription tiers include access to attorney consultations — something Bizee simply doesn't offer in a comparable way. If you anticipate needing ongoing legal guidance (reviewing contracts, navigating employment questions, handling disputes), LegalZoom's attorney network is a meaningful differentiator.

In my experience reviewing these platforms since 2021, this is the single most undervalued feature in LegalZoom's lineup. Most people skip it — until they need it.

### Business Dashboard and Post-Formation Tools

Both platforms offer an online dashboard for tracking your LLC's status, storing documents, and managing compliance deadlines. Bizee's dashboard is clean and functional; LegalZoom's has more integrations and a more mature compliance calendar. For 2026, both have mobile-friendly interfaces.

## Turnaround Time and Filing Speed

Filing speed depends heavily on the state, not just the service — but the formation company's internal processing time matters too.

**Standard filing:**
- Bizee: typically 2–3 business days to submit to the state
- LegalZoom: typically 3–5 business days to submit to the state

**Expedited filing:**
- Both services offer rush processing for an additional fee ($50–$100 depending on the plan and state)
- State processing times then apply on top of that (e.g., California takes 3–5 business days; Texas is often 1–2 business days)

For most founders, the difference between 2 and 5 business days doesn't materially affect their launch timeline. But if you're racing a contract deadline or a bank account opening, Bizee's slightly faster standard turnaround is a point in its favor.

## Hidden Costs: The Numbers They Don't Lead With

One of the most important things to understand about LLC formation services in 2026 is that the headline price is rarely the total price. Here's where costs accumulate:

**State filing fees** — always separate, always required. These range from $50 in states like Kentucky to $520 in Massachusetts. California charges an $800 minimum annual franchise tax on top of the $70 filing fee. [See our full cost breakdown](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-form-an-llc/) before you choose your state.

**Registered agent renewal** — as noted, Bizee charges $119/year vs LegalZoom's $249/year after the first free year. This is often the biggest surprise cost for new LLC owners.

**EIN filing** — free on mid-tier plans, but $70–$80 as an add-on if you start with the free Silver/Basic plan.

**Operating agreement** — free on mid-tier plans, but $99–$125 as an add-on on base plans.

**Annual report filing** — some states require annual or biennial reports with filing fees. Both services offer to handle this for an additional fee (~$100–$150/year). You can also file these yourself directly with the state for free (you pay only the state fee).

**BOI Report** — under the Corporate Transparency Act, most LLCs formed after January 1, 2024 are required to file a Beneficial Ownership Information report. Both Bizee and LegalZoom will file this for you for an additional fee. You can also file it yourself at no cost via [FinCEN's online portal](https://boiefiling.fincen.gov/). For more on deadlines and requirements, see our [BOI Report Guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Incfile and Bizee the same company?**
Yes. Incfile rebranded to Bizee in 2023. The company, ownership, platform, and service offerings are the same — only the name and branding changed. If you formed an LLC with Incfile, your account is now under the Bizee brand.

**Is Bizee really free to form an LLC?**
Bizee charges $0 in service fees on its Silver plan, but you still pay your state's filing fee — which ranges from $50 to $520 depending on the state. The "free" refers only to Bizee's preparation and submission fee, not the government costs.

**How much does LegalZoom charge for LLC formation in 2026?**
LegalZoom's base (Basic) plan starts at $0 in service fees plus state fees, just like Bizee. Its Pro plan is $249 and its Premium plan is $299, both plus state fees.

**Which is faster: Bizee or LegalZoom?**
Bizee generally processes and submits filings slightly faster — typically 2–3 business days vs LegalZoom's 3–5 days before state processing begins. Both offer expedited options for an additional fee.

**Do I need a registered agent if I use Bizee or LegalZoom?**
Yes — every LLC in every state is legally required to have a registered agent. Both services include one free year of registered agent service. After that, you can continue with them, switch to another provider, or act as your own registered agent in states that allow it.

**What happens after the first free year of registered agent service?**
Bizee charges $119/year and LegalZoom charges $249/year for continued registered agent service. You can switch to a different registered agent (like Northwest at $125/year) or, if your state allows it and you have a physical street address, designate yourself.

**Is LegalZoom worth the extra cost over Bizee?**
It depends on your needs. If you want attorney consultation access or anticipate ongoing legal document needs, LegalZoom's premium plans can be worth the premium. For straightforward formations with no anticipated legal complexity, Bizee delivers equivalent formation outcomes at a lower cost.

**Can I switch from Bizee to LegalZoom (or vice versa) after formation?**
The formation itself is done — once your LLC is filed with the state, the formation service's role is largely complete. You can switch your registered agent at any time. Any post-formation services (annual reports, legal documents) can be handled by a different provider going forward.
