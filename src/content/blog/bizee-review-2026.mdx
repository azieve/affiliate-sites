## What Is Bizee? The Incfile Rebrand Explained

Bizee was founded in 2004 under the name Incfile, making it one of the first companies to automate LLC formation online at scale. For nearly two decades, the company built its reputation on a single value proposition: file your LLC for free and let us handle the paperwork. By the early 2020s, Incfile had processed over a million business formations — a volume that put it among the largest formation services in the country by sheer order count.

In 2023, the company rebranded to [Bizee](/go/bizee/). The stated rationale was broader positioning as a full-service small business platform, not just a filing utility. The rebrand brought a refreshed dashboard, updated branding, and some new add-on service categories (bookkeeping, taxes, business banking partnerships). The core LLC formation product, however, remained essentially unchanged.

As of mid-2026, Bizee operates in all 50 states and still anchors its marketing around the free Silver plan. Whether the Incfile rebrand created a meaningfully better product or primarily served as a marketing refresh is a fair question — and one this review addresses directly.

## Bizee Pricing and Plans in 2026: What Each Tier Gets You

Bizee offers three plan tiers. All prices below are **Bizee's service fees only** — your state's filing fee applies on top of every plan. State fees range from $40 in Kentucky and $50 in New Mexico to $500 in Massachusetts and $420 in Tennessee, so factor your specific state into the total cost calculation.

**Silver — $0 service fee**

- Preparation and filing of articles of organization
- Unlimited business name searches
- Free registered agent service for 1 year
- Online order status tracking
- Lifetime company alerts and annual report reminders
- Email and chat support

**Gold — $199/year**

- Everything in Silver, plus:
- EIN (Employer Identification Number) obtainment from the IRS
- IRS Form 2553 (S-Corp tax election filing)
- Banking resolution document
- Customized operating agreement
- Unlimited phone support

**Platinum — $299/year**

- Everything in Gold, plus:
- Business contract templates library
- Expedited filing service
- Business domain name and email address
- Business website

**The number that matters most:** After year one, registered agent service auto-renews at **$119/year**. This is the single most important cost detail in any Bizee pricing review. The free plan stops being free in year two — and if you forget to cancel or switch, the charge hits automatically. Every new Bizee customer should calendar a reminder for 11 months after signup to evaluate their options.

## Bizee Features: What Works, What Doesn't, and the Hidden Costs

### What Bizee Does Well

The Silver plan delivers on its core promise. Bizee prepares and submits your articles of organization accurately, and the online dashboard is clean, easy to navigate, and genuinely useful for tracking your order status. You get compliance reminders for state annual report deadlines, which is more than some free-tier competitors bother to include.

Formation processing times are competitive. Bizee's internal processing typically runs 1–3 business days before submission to the state. After that, state processing times govern the real timeline: Wyoming and New Mexico often approve in 1–2 days, while California can take 4–6 weeks under normal conditions. Bizee's Platinum tier offers expedited handling on their end, but no service can accelerate state-side processing.

Bizee also offers a growing ecosystem of business services — bookkeeping, tax preparation, trademark registration — that can be added on individually after formation. These aren't included features, but they do mean you can centralize several business admin tasks in one vendor if that's appealing.

### Where Bizee Falls Short

**Operating agreement locked behind Gold.** A [LLC operating agreement](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) isn't optional paperwork — it's the document that defines ownership percentages, decision-making authority, and what happens when a member exits the business. Bizee withholds this from the free Silver plan. [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) includes an operating agreement even on its entry-level plan. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) bundles it with the Pro tier at $199/year alongside EIN, compliance guarantee, and registered agent — making it a more complete value at the same price point as Bizee Gold.

**No EIN on the free plan.** An Employer Identification Number is required to open a business bank account, hire employees, or file taxes as anything other than a sole proprietor. Getting one yourself through the [IRS EIN online application](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) is free and takes about 10 minutes if you have a U.S. SSN — but Bizee's Silver plan doesn't walk you through it or handle it for you. That's a meaningful gap for first-time founders who don't know the EIN step even exists.

**Customer support tiers.** Email and chat are Silver's only support channels. Based on user reviews aggregated from Trustpilot and the Better Business Bureau, response times on email can lag during high-volume periods. Unlimited phone support only unlocks at Gold ($199). The expectation that phone access requires a $200 commitment is a friction point — competitors like ZenBusiness include responsive chat and phone support at lower price thresholds.

**Checkout upsell density.** After selecting your base plan, Bizee's order flow presents multiple add-on prompts: registered agent upgrades, business license research, tax consultations, website packages. None of these are dishonest services, but the checkout experience can feel cluttered when you're just trying to get articles of organization filed. This is common across the industry, but Bizee's flow is heavier than most.

In my experience researching and testing formation services, the "free LLC" pitch is almost always a door-opener — and that's not inherently deceptive. The real question is whether the door it opens is worth walking through. For Bizee, the Silver plan is genuinely functional for experienced founders who know how to obtain their own EIN and don't need an operating agreement immediately. For everyone else, the math quickly points toward a $199 plan, at which point the comparison to ZenBusiness Pro becomes the deciding question.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use Bizee in 2026?

**Bizee works well for:**

- Experienced founders forming a second or third LLC who already understand the compliance requirements and can self-navigate EIN registration and operating agreement drafting
- Solo operators building a simple single-member LLC with no immediate need for an operating agreement
- Budget-constrained entrepreneurs who need formation only — not ongoing support — and intend to switch registered agents after year one
- Founders who previously used Incfile and want account continuity through the rebrand

**Bizee is a poor fit for:**

- Multi-member LLCs that need a proper operating agreement from day one (the Silver plan doesn't include one)
- S-Corp election candidates who need IRS Form 2553 filed promptly after formation (Gold plan required)
- Entrepreneurs who want proactive compliance monitoring and guaranteed annual report filing
- Business owners operating across multiple states, where Bizee's multi-state tools are limited compared to competitors

The U.S. Small Business Administration [advises](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure) that new LLCs obtain an EIN and formalize their ownership structure before opening a business bank account. Both of those steps fall outside Bizee's free plan. That's a meaningful gap for first-time founders who may not realize those steps are required at all.

Understanding what a registered agent does — and what it costs to maintain that service year over year — is also essential before committing. ## People Also Ask: Bizee FAQ

**Is Bizee's free LLC formation actually free?**

The $0 service fee is genuine. Bizee doesn't charge a markup for processing on the Silver plan. However, you still pay your state's filing fee, which varies from $40 (Kentucky) to $500 (Massachusetts). You also pay $119/year for registered agent service starting in year two. "Free" accurately describes the service fee, not the total cost of forming and maintaining your LLC.

**What happened to Incfile? Is Bizee the same company?**

Yes. Incfile rebranded to Bizee in 2023. The company, founding team, and core formation service are the same. The rebrand introduced updated branding and some product enhancements but didn't change the fundamental offering. If you formed an LLC through Incfile, your company records and account are now accessible through Bizee's platform.

**How much does Bizee's registered agent cost after year 1?**

Registered agent service renews at $119/year after the complimentary first year. If you decide to switch to a different registered agent provider, you'll need to file a registered agent change with your state — typically a modest fee of $10–$50 depending on the state.

**Does Bizee include an operating agreement?**

Not on the Silver (free) plan. Operating agreements are included starting with the Gold plan at $199/year. If you're forming a multi-member LLC, you should have an operating agreement in place before the business opens a bank account or takes on any liability. On the Silver plan, you'd need to draft one yourself, work with an attorney, or upgrade to Gold.

**How does Bizee compare to ZenBusiness?**

At the $0 tier, both services offer nearly identical value: articles of organization filing with a free first-year registered agent. The gap widens at $199/year, where ZenBusiness Pro includes a compliance guarantee and stronger customer support infrastructure alongside the same EIN and operating agreement that Bizee Gold provides. Most independent comparisons — including ours — place ZenBusiness ahead at the $199 tier.

**Does Bizee help with BOI reporting?**

Yes. Bizee offers BOI (Beneficial Ownership Information) report filing as a paid add-on. Following the Corporate Transparency Act regulations enforced through FinCEN, most LLCs are required to file a BOI report disclosing their beneficial owners. Penalties for non-filing reach $591 per day. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) explains who must file, the current deadlines, and how to complete the filing yourself at no cost through the FinCEN portal.

**How long does Bizee take to form an LLC?**

Bizee's internal processing takes 1–3 business days before your documents are submitted to the state. After that, state processing governs the real timeline: Wyoming and New Mexico often approve within 1–2 business days; California and New York can take several weeks under normal volume. Bizee's Platinum plan includes expedited internal processing, but cannot accelerate the state's review timeline.

**Is Bizee a legitimate company?**

Yes. Bizee (formerly Incfile) has been operating since 2004 and holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. The company has processed over a million LLC formations across all 50 states. The legitimacy question is settled — the relevant question is whether Bizee is the right fit for your specific formation needs relative to available alternatives.

At $0, it competes directly with Bizee Silver. At $199/year, ZenBusiness Pro delivers a more complete bundle than Bizee Gold. And the long-term registered agent and compliance infrastructure that comes with ZenBusiness is meaningfully stronger.

The Incfile rebrand to Bizee didn't close that gap. It refreshed the brand without fundamentally upgrading the product. That's not a fatal flaw — Bizee remains a solid, trustworthy formation service — but it means that for most readers comparing options in 2026, Bizee is best positioned as a budget choice for those who know exactly what they need, not a full-service recommendation for founders building a business from scratch.
