If you are weighing Swyft Filings vs ZenBusiness, you are probably trying to answer one practical question: which company will get your LLC filed correctly, at a fair all-in price, with the least follow-up work? Both are online formation services, both offer a $0 service-fee entry tier (you still pay your state's filing fee), and both upsell you into paid bundles. The differences show up in what those bundles include, how much you pay in year two, and how much support you get after the LLC exists.

My short answer for most first-time owners: [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) is the safer default in 2026. It has a clearer tier structure, a larger compliance toolkit (annual report reminders, a registered agent, a bank-account and EIN workflow), and a long track record. Swyft Filings is a credible lower-cost option worth a look if you want a no-frills filing and are comfortable handling compliance yourself. Details below, so you can decide with numbers rather than marketing.

## Pricing and Hidden Costs: What You Actually Pay

The headline price is the least useful number on either site. What matters is the total for year one and year two.

**ZenBusiness** structures its offering as Starter ($0 plus state fee), Pro ($199 per year), and Premium ($349 per year). Starter files your Articles of Organization and little else. The Pro tier adds the operating agreement, EIN assistance, a registered agent, and a business website/domain offer. Premium adds faster processing and extras like a business license report and additional compliance tools. The catch many owners miss: bundled items like registered agent service are often free for the first year and then billed at a renewal rate. Budget for that.

**Swyft Filings** follows a similar $0 / mid / top structure. The $0 tier files your paperwork, and the paid bundles layer on the registered agent, operating agreement, and EIN. Our [Swyft Filings review](/blog/swyft-filings-review/) walks through the specific add-on prices. The same rule applies: check what is free only in year one.

Here is a worked example. Say you form a single-member LLC in Texas, where the state filing fee is $300. With a $0 tier from either company, your day-one cost is the $300 state fee. But then you need a registered agent (Texas requires one), so you either serve as your own agent or buy that service. If you choose a mid-tier bundle, the formation total rises accordingly, and the question becomes what you pay at renewal. That is the number I always ask owners to model before they commit.

In my experience, the cheapest formation price is rarely the cheapest outcome. I have seen too many business owners pick a $0 tier, then spend hours and extra money on an EIN, an operating agreement, and a missed annual report penalty that a mid-tier bundle would have prevented.

## Features Compared: Formation, Registered Agent and Operating Agreement

**Formation filing.** Both services prepare and submit your Articles of Organization to the state. Speed depends mostly on the state's processing time, not the provider, though paid tiers from both companies usually offer rush options.

**Registered agent.** Every LLC needs one in the state where it is formed. You can learn more in our guide to [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/). Both companies include the service in paid bundles for a period, and both will charge to continue. If privacy and registered agent quality are your priority, [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) is the specialist worth considering. For a head-to-head, see [Northwest vs ZenBusiness](/compare/northwest-vs-zenbusiness/).

**Operating agreement.** An operating agreement is not filed with the state, but it matters for liability protection and for banks. Both providers supply a template at higher tiers. Templates are generic: if you have partners, a unique profit split, or any buy-sell concerns, have an attorney review the document. Our [operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to include.

**EIN.** You can get an Employer Identification Number directly from the IRS for free, as explained on the [IRS EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers). Paying a service to obtain it is a convenience, not a necessity. Do not let any provider make you feel an EIN requires a paid plan.

**Compliance after formation.** This is where ZenBusiness pulls ahead in my view. Its annual report reminders and filing assistance reduce the risk of falling out of good standing, which can lead to fines or administrative dissolution. Swyft Filings offers less here, so you would need your own calendar system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Swyft Filings better than ZenBusiness?
For most first-time owners, no. ZenBusiness offers a more complete ongoing compliance toolkit and a longer track record. Swyft Filings may appeal to owners prioritizing a low upfront price who will handle compliance themselves.

### How much does it cost to form an LLC with ZenBusiness?
ZenBusiness's Starter plan has a $0 service fee, plus your state's filing fee. Its Pro plan is $199 per year and Premium is $349 per year. Always confirm current pricing at checkout, since it changes.

### Do I need a formation service to start an LLC?
No. You can file directly with your state's Secretary of State, usually for just the state fee. Formation services add convenience, templates, and compliance reminders. The SBA's [guide to LLCs](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure) explains the self-filing basics.

### Is the $0 LLC formation plan really free?
The service fee is $0, but you still pay the state filing fee, which ranges from roughly $35 to $500. You will also likely need a registered agent, which may cost extra after any included period.

### Do I need to pay for an EIN?
No. The IRS issues EINs for free. Paying a service for EIN help is optional.

### What happens if I forget my annual report?
Many states charge late fees and may eventually administratively dissolve your LLC, which can strip your liability protection until you reinstate. Reminder tools from a provider can help, but the responsibility remains yours.

### Can I switch from Swyft Filings to ZenBusiness later?
Yes. You can change your registered agent and move compliance services at any time. Your LLC itself stays registered with the state; you only update the agent and service provider details.

### Should I use an attorney instead?
If you have multiple owners, significant assets, or a regulated profession, an attorney-reviewed setup is worth the added cost. Consult a qualified professional about your specific situation.
