## What Is Swyft Filings?

Swyft Filings is an online LLC formation and registered agent service that handles the paperwork of starting a business in all 50 states. They prepare and file your Articles of Organization with the state, provide document templates, and offer add-on compliance services like EIN registration, operating agreements, and registered agent coverage.

Their core pitch is simplicity: answer a few questions about your business, select a plan, pay (or don't, on the basic tier), and Swyft handles the state filing. For many first-time entrepreneurs who have never started an LLC before, that guided experience is genuinely valuable.

Swyft Filings claims to have helped over one million businesses get started — a credible figure given their decade-plus in the market. They carry an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau, which is reassuring. But rating agencies measure complaint resolution, not value for money. The more granular question is whether their plans are priced competitively and whether the experience lives up to the promise.

## Swyft Filings Pricing: What You'll Actually Pay

Understanding Swyft Filings pricing requires you to separate the service fee from the unavoidable state filing fee. Every LLC formation service charges state fees on top of its own price — and those fees vary by state (Delaware is $90, California is $70, Texas is $300, Wyoming is just $100). None of that goes to Swyft.

| **Standard** | ~$149 | Everything in Basic + EIN registration, operating agreement template, banking resolution, expedited processing |

The "free" Basic plan sounds compelling, but it comes with meaningful limitations: you get standard (slow) processing, no EIN assistance, and no registered agent service. You'll need a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) in every state you're doing business — that's a legal requirement, not optional. If you add registered agent service separately on the Basic plan, your effective cost is already $149–$199, which puts you in range of competitors' full-featured tiers.

## Swyft Filings Plans: Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

### Basic Plan ($0 + state fees)

The Basic plan covers the essential mechanics: Swyft prepares and files your Articles of Organization, checks name availability, and delivers your formation documents digitally. Processing follows standard state timelines, which range from a few days (Wyoming, New Mexico) to several weeks (California, New York).

What you don't get: an EIN (Employer Identification Number), an operating agreement, registered agent service, or any expedited processing. You'll need to obtain an EIN directly from the IRS — which is free but requires navigating the [IRS EIN application](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) on your own. And you'll need to source a registered agent independently, which typically costs $100–$300/year through a third-party service.

For a solo freelancer or side business owner who is comfortable handling those steps manually, the Basic plan does work. For everyone else, it's an incomplete solution.

### Standard Plan (~$149 + state fees)

The Standard plan is where Swyft Filings becomes meaningfully useful. You get EIN registration (Swyft handles the IRS paperwork), a customizable operating agreement template, a banking resolution letter (needed to open a business bank account), and expedited document processing.

This is the tier most business owners actually need. The jump from $0 to $149 may feel significant, but when you factor in the time savings on EIN registration and operating agreement drafting, it's reasonable.

### Premium Plan (~$299 + state fees)

The Premium plan bundles one year of registered agent service with everything in Standard, plus rush processing and priority support access. If you're paying $149–$199 for standalone registered agent service anyway, the Premium plan's economics can make sense — especially if you want faster turnaround and dedicated support.

That said, $299 is a notable price point when compared to competitors who include registered agent service in lower-priced tiers.

## Hidden Fees and Upsells to Watch Out For

In my experience evaluating LLC formation services, the checkout upsell experience tells you almost as much about a company as the plan features themselves. Swyft Filings is known for an aggressive upsell flow.

Common add-ons you'll encounter during checkout include:

- **Certified copy of Articles of Organization:** ~$30–$50 (often required by banks, but Swyft charges extra)
- **Annual report compliance alerts:** ~$99–$149/year (reminders you can set up yourself for free)
- **Business license research:** ~$99 (this is a report, not actual license filing)
- **Employer State ID:** Additional fee on top of EIN
- **Virtual address / mail forwarding:** ~$99–$149/year

None of these are inherently dishonest, but the cumulative effect at checkout can push your total well beyond what you expected from a "$0" starting price. Some users in online forums and on Trustpilot note that they felt pressured into add-ons they didn't fully understand — a common complaint worth taking seriously before you begin the checkout process.

The IRS and SBA both offer free resources explaining which of these services are genuinely necessary for most small LLCs — and spoiler: several of these add-ons are optional or can be obtained at lower cost elsewhere. According to the [Small Business Administration](https://www.sba.gov/), the core requirements for a functional LLC are Articles of Organization, an EIN, an operating agreement, and a registered agent. Everything else is situational.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use Swyft Filings

### Consider Swyft Filings if:

- **You want a basic filing and nothing else.** If you're comfortable navigating the IRS EIN process yourself, sourcing your own registered agent, and drafting your own operating agreement, Swyft's $0 Basic plan gets the essential state filing done.
- **You're in a low-complexity state.** Wyoming, New Mexico, or Montana have simple filing requirements and low fees. A bare-bones formation service may be all you need.
- **You've already budgeted the add-ons.** If you go in with eyes open about the upsells and pick exactly what you need, Swyft's Standard plan at $149 is in line with mid-market pricing.

### Skip Swyft Filings if:

- **You want all-in-one convenience.** The need to piece together registered agent, EIN, and operating agreement from multiple add-ons is friction that competitors eliminate at comparable or lower prices.
- **You're forming in a complex state.** California, New York, and Illinois have additional requirements (publication rules, franchise taxes, biennial statements) where strong compliance support matters. ZenBusiness or Northwest's more robust platforms are better suited.
- **Customer service responsiveness matters to you.** Swyft Filings is an email-first support operation on lower tiers. For a business formation process where a missed step can delay your LLC for weeks, that's a real risk.
- **You're starting a multi-member LLC.** More complex ownership structures benefit from professional operating agreement support that goes beyond a basic template.

For most readers — especially those forming their first LLC — I've consistently pointed them toward [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) as the starting point. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does Swyft Filings actually cost?

Swyft Filings pricing starts at $0 for its Basic plan (plus your state's filing fee), roughly $149 for its Standard plan, and approximately $299 for its Premium plan. However, the total cost rises quickly with add-ons like registered agent service (~$149–$199/year), certified copies, and compliance alerts. Always calculate your all-in cost before choosing a plan.

### Is Swyft Filings legitimate?

Yes. Swyft Filings is a legitimate LLC formation service that has been operating since 2012 and holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau. They file real Articles of Organization with real state agencies. User complaints tend to focus on aggressive upselling and customer service response times, not on fraudulent activity.

### Does Swyft Filings include a registered agent?

Not unless you're on the Premium plan or pay for it as a separate add-on. The Basic and Standard plans do not include registered agent service. You are legally required to have a registered agent in every state where your LLC operates, so factor this cost into your budget from the start. See our guide on [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) for more detail.

When comparing Swyft Filings vs. ZenBusiness, ZenBusiness consistently comes out ahead on value. ### How long does Swyft Filings take to file an LLC?

On the Basic plan, Swyft Filings processes your filing on standard state timelines, which range from same-day (some states) to 3–6 weeks (California, New York).
