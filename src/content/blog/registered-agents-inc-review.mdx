## What Registered Agents Inc Actually Does

A registered agent is the person or company designated to receive legal documents (service of process) and official state correspondence on behalf of your LLC. Every state requires one. The agent needs a physical street address in the state where the LLC is registered and must be available during normal business hours.

Registered Agents Inc typically offers these core services:

- **Registered agent service** in all 50 states, with document scanning and forwarding
- **LLC and corporation formation**, including filings in popular states like Wyoming and Delaware
- **Compliance support**, such as annual report reminders
- **Mail forwarding and related business support**, depending on the plan

The company's strongest reputation is for the registered agent function itself. That is the one thing you cannot easily do yourself if you travel often or work from home. Using your home address as the agent address puts your address on a public record, which many owners want to avoid. ## What Else You Need Beyond a Registered Agent

A registered agent is one piece of the setup. Whichever provider you choose, plan for the following:

- **Operating agreement.** Even single-member LLCs benefit from one. Read our [operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/).
- **EIN.** You can get one free directly from the IRS. See the [IRS page on applying for an EIN](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) and never pay a third party for the number itself.
- **Annual or biennial reports.** Missing them can lead to penalties or dissolution. Our [guide to what happens if you don't renew](/blog/what-happens-if-you-don-t-renew-your-llc/) covers the consequences.
- **Beneficial ownership reporting.** In March 2025, FinCEN issued an interim final rule removing the BOI reporting requirement for U.S.-formed companies and their owners. Rules in this area have shifted repeatedly, so check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status, and see our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/).
- **Bank account and bookkeeping.** Keep business and personal funds separate to protect your liability shield.

The U.S. Small Business Administration also publishes a helpful overview of [business structures and registration](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure) if you want a neutral government source.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Registered Agents Inc legit?

Yes. It is an established registered agent and formation company based in Wyoming. As with any provider, read the terms, check the renewal pricing, and confirm what is included before you pay.

### How much does Registered Agents Inc cost?

Pricing varies by state and service. Registered agent fees commonly fall in the low-to-mid $100s per year, and formation services add a fee on top of state filing costs. Confirm the current quote on the company's website.

### Is Registered Agents Inc the same as Northwest Registered Agent?

No. They are separate companies with similar names. Northwest Registered Agent is a different provider, and you can read our [Northwest review](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) for details.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in the state. You can serve as your own agent, but that puts your name and address on public record. See our [guide to registered agents](/blog/what-is-a-registered-agent-and-do-i-need-one/).

### Can I switch registered agents later?

Yes. You file a change of registered agent form with the state, usually for a small fee, and name the new agent. Our [step-by-step guide](/blog/how-to-change-registered-agent/) explains the process.

### What happens if my registered agent fails to forward a lawsuit?

If a registered agent misses service of process, you may lose the chance to respond, and a court could enter a default judgment against your LLC. This is why reliability and notification speed matter more than the lowest price.

### Is it cheaper to use a formation service or a standalone registered agent?

For a new LLC, a bundled formation service is usually cheaper in year one because the registered agent is included. Over the long run, compare renewal rates. Our [cheapest registered agent service roundup](/blog/cheapest-registered-agent-service/) can help.

### Can I use Registered Agents Inc for an LLC in a state where I don't live?

Generally yes, and that is a common reason owners use a commercial registered agent. You still need to meet that state's registration and tax requirements. See our guide to [foreign LLC registration](/blog/foreign-llc-registration-doing-business-in-another-state/).
