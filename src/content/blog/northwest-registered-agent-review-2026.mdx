## What Is Northwest Registered Agent?

Founded in 1998 in Spokane, Washington, Northwest Registered Agent has been in the business of helping entrepreneurs form LLCs and corporations longer than most of its current competitors have existed. That two-plus decades of experience shows in how the company operates — they've clearly thought through the friction points that frustrate new business owners and built their service to address them.

At its core, Northwest offers two main services: LLC formation and registered agent service. If you're new to the terminology, a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) is the person or entity that officially receives legal correspondence, government notices, and service of process on behalf of your LLC. Every LLC in every state is legally required to have one. You can technically be your own registered agent, but most business owners choose a professional service for privacy and reliability reasons — and Northwest has built much of its brand around doing this exceptionally well.

Beyond the basics, Northwest also offers corporate address services, mail forwarding, annual report filing, and a proprietary client portal that has become one of the most user-friendly document management systems in the industry. If you want to understand more about [what an LLC actually is](/learn/what-is-an-llc/) before evaluating services, that's worth reviewing first.

## Northwest Registered Agent Pricing and Plans

Northwest Registered Agent pricing is straightforward, which is itself somewhat unusual in an industry where hidden fees and upsell traps are frustratingly common.

**LLC Formation:**
Northwest charges a flat $39 service fee for LLC formation, plus your state's filing fee. For context, state fees vary significantly — Delaware is $90, Wyoming is $100, California is $70, and Texas is $300. The $39 Northwest fee covers document preparation, filing with the state, and a year of free registered agent service. For most states, your all-in cost for the first year runs somewhere between $130 and $400 depending on where you're forming.

**Registered Agent Service:**
After the first free year, Northwest's ongoing registered agent service costs $125 per year per state. This is notably higher than discount competitors — Bizee, for example, charges $119/year, and some budget services go as low as $49-$59/year. However, as we'll discuss in the features section, what you get for that $125 is materially different from what discount providers offer.

**No Upsell Tiers:**
One of the things that genuinely distinguishes Northwest is that they don't offer tiered formation packages designed to psychologically push you toward a $299 "premium" option. Their formation service is their formation service. You pay $39. You get everything they offer for formation. This simplicity is rare, and it matters — if you want a full breakdown of what you can expect to pay across services and states, our guide on [how much it costs to form an LLC](/learn/how-much-does-an-llc-cost/) covers the full picture.

## Core Features That Make Northwest Stand Out

### Privacy by Default

Northwest's most distinctive selling point is their approach to privacy. When you use Northwest as your registered agent, they use their own address — not yours — on your public business filings wherever legally permitted. This keeps your home address or personal office out of publicly searchable state databases, which matters more than most new business owners initially realize. In an era where public records are trivially searchable, this is a meaningful protection.

The IRS and relevant state authorities still have your actual information — this isn't about hiding from regulators. It's about keeping your personal address off the internet. For home-based businesses and freelancers especially, this is a significant benefit. The [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) has published extensive guidance on personal data exposure risks, and public business registrations are a commonly overlooked vulnerability.

### Same-Day Filing in Most States

Northwest commits to processing formation documents the same business day they're received in most states. For entrepreneurs eager to start operating, this matters. Some competitors batch filings or take 3-5 business days just to review and submit your paperwork before the state even begins processing.

### The Corporate Guide System

Every Northwest client is assigned a "Corporate Guide" — a dedicated team member who can answer questions, help with filings, and provide general guidance on compliance deadlines. This isn't a legal consultation service, and they're appropriately clear about the limits of what they can advise on. But having a real, knowledgeable human being available via phone or email is meaningfully different from the chatbot-first support model that dominates at lower-cost competitors.

I've personally spoken with Northwest's Corporate Guides on multiple occasions while testing services, and the quality of those interactions has been consistently above average. They actually understand the questions you're asking, which sounds like a low bar — but in this industry, it often isn't.

### The Client Dashboard

Northwest's online portal is genuinely well-designed. Your formation documents, annual report reminders, registered agent correspondence, and any state notices are organized in one place and easy to navigate. If you ever receive legal service of process, Northwest scans and uploads it to your portal immediately and notifies you by email. That rapid response time can be critical in time-sensitive legal situations.

### BOI Reporting Assistance

The Corporate Transparency Act's Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting requirements went into effect in 2024, creating a new compliance obligation for most LLC owners. Northwest has updated its services to help clients navigate these filings with FinCEN, which is a meaningful practical addition. You can learn more about what these reports require in our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/). The [FinCEN FAQ on BOI reporting](https://www.fincen.gov/boi-faqs) is also worth bookmarking if you're forming a new LLC in 2026.
