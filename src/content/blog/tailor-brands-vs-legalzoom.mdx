## What Each Service Actually Does

An LLC formation service is a paid filing agent. It prepares your Articles of Organization, submits them to the Secretary of State, and layers on extras such as a registered agent, EIN help and an operating agreement. You can file directly with your state, and our guide on [how much an LLC costs](/learn/how-much-does-an-llc-cost/) shows the do-it-yourself baseline. The services sell convenience and bundled compliance.

### Tailor Brands LLC

[Tailor Brands](/go/tailor-brands/) began as an AI logo maker and expanded into business formation. Its pitch is a single dashboard for your brand identity, website and LLC filing. The Tailor Brands LLC packages typically start with a low-or-no service-fee tier plus state fees, then step up through paid tiers that add things like EIN assistance, an operating agreement template and brand assets.

Our [Tailor Brands LLC review](/blog/tailor-brands-llc-review/) covers the tiers in detail. The strengths are design tools and a simple interface. The weaknesses are thinner legal and compliance support than the dedicated formation specialists offer.

### LegalZoom LLC Formation

[LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) is one of the best-known names in online legal services. Its LegalZoom LLC formation packages typically run from a basic tier (service fee of $0 plus state fees) through mid and top tiers that add an operating agreement, EIN, expedited processing and a year of registered agent service. It also sells wills, trademarks, contracts and attorney consultations, which is the main reason people choose it. See our full [LegalZoom review](/reviews/legalzoom/) for more.

## Tax and Compliance Considerations

No formation service replaces tax planning. A single-member LLC is generally taxed as a disregarded entity by default, so profits flow to your personal return, and self-employment tax applies. Multi-member LLCs default to partnership treatment. Some owners later elect S-corp status to potentially reduce self-employment tax once profits are high enough, but that election involves payroll and reasonable-salary rules. Read the [IRS guidance on LLCs](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc) and our [LLC vs S-Corp](/learn/llc-vs-s-corp/) article, and talk to a CPA.

On federal reporting, FinCEN's Beneficial Ownership Information rules changed substantially in 2025. Under an interim final rule issued in March 2025, domestic companies were exempted from BOI reporting. Rules can change again, so check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) and our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) before assuming anything in 2026.

State compliance is where owners slip. Annual reports, franchise taxes and registered agent upkeep keep your LLC in good standing, and missing them can lead to administrative dissolution. This is a place where a provider with built-in reminders earns its fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Tailor Brands or LegalZoom better for forming an LLC?

It depends on your priorities. [Tailor Brands](/go/tailor-brands/) is better for founders who want branding tools bundled in. [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) is better if you want attorney access and a wider range of legal documents. For overall value, many founders prefer [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/).

### How much does it cost to form an LLC with LegalZoom?

You pay the state filing fee plus the package's service fee. LegalZoom's basic tier has typically been $0 plus state fees, with higher tiers priced in the low-to-mid hundreds. Add-ons and renewals raise the total, so confirm current pricing at checkout.

### Is Tailor Brands legit for LLC formation?

Yes, it is a real, operating filing service. The main caution is that its strengths are in branding rather than deep legal or compliance support, so check what each tier includes.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent. You can serve as your own, but a service keeps your address off public records and ensures someone is available during business hours. See our [registered agent guide](/blog/what-is-a-registered-agent-and-do-i-need-one/).

### Can I form an LLC myself without a service?

Yes. You can file directly with your Secretary of State, usually for just the state fee. Services add convenience, bundled extras and reminders. Compare costs in our [cheapest way to form an LLC](/blog/cheapest-way-to-form-an-llc/) guide.

### What happens if I forget my annual report?

Penalties vary by state, but missed annual reports can lead to late fees, loss of good standing and eventually administrative dissolution. Choose a provider with compliance reminders, or set your own calendar alerts.

### Does an LLC protect my personal assets?

An LLC can separate business liabilities from personal assets, but protection isn't absolute. Courts may disregard it if you mix personal and business funds or ignore formalities, and it doesn't cover your own negligence. Consult an attorney for your situation.

### Which LLC service is the cheapest?

Several services advertise $0 service fees, including [Bizee](/go/bizee/) and [Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/), but add-ons can change the total. Look at the full cost over at least two years.
