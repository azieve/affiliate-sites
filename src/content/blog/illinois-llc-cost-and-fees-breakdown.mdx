## Illinois LLC Filing Fee: The $150 Articles of Organization

The core of any Illinois LLC cost and fees breakdown starts with the Articles of Organization, filed as Form LLC-5.5 with the [Illinois Secretary of State's Department of Business Services](https://www.ilsos.gov/departments/business_services/home.html). The filing fee is a flat $150 whether you file online or by mail, and it's the same regardless of your LLC's size, industry, or number of members.

Standard processing typically takes around 7–10 business days for mail filings, and online filings are usually processed faster. If you're in a hurry — say, you need a bank account open by Friday — Illinois offers 24-hour expedited processing for an additional $100, bringing your total state cost to $250. That's a meaningful premium, so I'd only recommend paying for expedited service if you have a real deadline (a signed lease, an investor requiring an EIN, a contract that needs a business entity behind it).

If you're not ready to file immediately but want to lock in your business name, Illinois lets you reserve it for 90 days using Form LLC-1.15 for a $25 fee. It's optional and most people skip it, but if you're still finalizing your operating agreement or waiting on a trademark search, it's cheap insurance against someone else grabbing your name first.

## Illinois LLC Annual Report Fee: $75 Every Year

This is the fee that catches people off guard, because it's easy to treat LLC formation as a one-time expense. It isn't. Illinois requires every LLC to file an annual report (Form LLC-50.1) and pay a $75 fee every single year to stay in good standing — a detail we cover in full in our [Illinois LLC annual report filing guide](/blog/illinois-llc-annual-report-filing/).

The report is due before the first day of your LLC's anniversary month — so if you formed your LLC in March, your annual report is due each year before March 1st. You can file up to 60 days early, and I'd genuinely recommend doing so. In my experience helping people troubleshoot compliance issues, missed annual reports are the single most common reason small Illinois LLCs slip out of good standing — not because owners don't care, but because the deadline isn't tied to a calendar-year date like taxes are, so it's easy to lose track.

Miss the deadline and Illinois tacks on a $100 late penalty. Ignore it long enough and the state can administratively dissolve your LLC, which means losing your liability protection and having to go through a reinstatement process to get it back — an entirely avoidable cost if you just calendar the date or let a registered agent service send you reminders.

## Registered Agent Costs in Illinois

Every Illinois LLC is legally required to designate a registered agent with a physical street address in Illinois (a P.O. box doesn't qualify) who is available during business hours to accept legal documents and state correspondence.

You have two real options:

**Serve as your own registered agent** — free, but it means your name and home or office address become part of the public record, and you need to be reliably present at that address during business hours. Miss a certified mail delivery for a lawsuit notice, and you could end up with a default judgment against you.

**Hire a commercial registered agent** — typically $99 to $300 per year depending on the provider. This keeps your personal address off public filings, gives you a professional point of contact for legal mail, and usually comes with compliance reminders so you don't miss that $75 annual report.

## Optional and Hidden Illinois LLC Costs

Beyond the mandatory line items, a few costs catch new owners by surprise:

- **Assumed name (DBA):** If you want to operate under a name different from your LLC's legal name, Illinois charges $150 to register an assumed name, and it must be renewed every 5 years for another $150.
- **Foreign LLC registration:** If you formed your LLC in another state (say, Wyoming for privacy reasons) but do business in Illinois, you'll need to register as a foreign LLC using Form LLC-45.5, another $150 fee, plus a Certificate of Good Standing from your home state.
- **EIN:** Getting an Employer Identification Number directly from the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers) is completely free. If a formation service charges you separately for this, you're paying for convenience, not a government fee.
- **Operating agreement:** Illinois doesn't require you to file one with the state, but skipping it is a mistake — without one, courts default to Illinois's generic LLC statute to settle member disputes, which rarely matches what you actually intended. Most formation services include a template for $0–$50; you can also review our [LLC operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) if you want to draft your own.
- **BOI reporting:** Under the Corporate Transparency Act, LLCs were originally required to file Beneficial Ownership Information reports with FinCEN. That changed in 2025 — FinCEN issued an interim final rule exempting U.S.-formed companies from BOI reporting entirely, narrowing the requirement to foreign entities registered to do business in the U.S. It's a fast-moving area of federal regulation, so check our [BOI report changes and updates for 2026](/blog/boi-report-changes-and-updates-2026/) before assuming you're fully in the clear, and confirm current status directly on [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi).

One genuinely good piece of news for this Illinois LLC cost and fees breakdown: unlike Illinois corporations, which were subject to a franchise tax based on paid-in capital before it was fully phased out by 2024, Illinois LLCs have never owed a state franchise tax. That alone makes the LLC structure noticeably cheaper to maintain long-term than a C-corp formed in the same state.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does it cost to start an LLC in Illinois in 2026?**
The mandatory state filing fee is $150 for the Articles of Organization. If you handle everything yourself with no add-ons, $150 is your total out-of-pocket cost to legally form the LLC. Ongoing costs include a $75 annual report fee every year.

**Do I need a registered agent for an Illinois LLC?**
Yes. Illinois law requires every LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical Illinois street address. You can serve as your own agent for free or pay a commercial service, typically $99–$300 per year, for added privacy and compliance reminders.

**What happens if I don't file my Illinois LLC annual report?**
You'll owe a $100 late penalty on top of the $75 fee, and continued non-compliance can lead to the state administratively dissolving your LLC, which strips away your liability protection until you go through reinstatement.

**Is there an Illinois LLC franchise tax?**
No. Unlike Illinois corporations, which paid a franchise tax based on paid-in capital before it was fully repealed by 2024, LLCs in Illinois have never been subject to a state franchise tax.

**Can I get an EIN for free?**
Yes. The IRS issues Employer Identification Numbers at no cost through its online application. Any formation service charging a separate "EIN fee" is charging for convenience, not a government requirement.

**Do I still need to file a BOI report for my Illinois LLC in 2026?**
Under a 2025 FinCEN interim final rule, most U.S.-formed companies, including domestic Illinois LLCs, are now exempt from Beneficial Ownership Information reporting. Foreign entities registered to do business in the U.S. still generally must file. Because this area of federal regulation has shifted quickly, confirm your current obligation on FinCEN's official BOI page before assuming you're exempt.

**Is it cheaper to use a formation service or file myself?**
Filing yourself is cheapest on paper ($150 total), but formation services like ZenBusiness often charge $0 plus the state fee for basic formation, meaning the cost difference is minimal while you gain guided filing, an operating agreement template, and compliance reminders that can save you from a $100 late penalty down the road.

**How long does it take to form an LLC in Illinois?**
Standard processing runs roughly 7–10 business days. Illinois offers 24-hour expedited processing for an additional $100 if you need your LLC formed faster.
