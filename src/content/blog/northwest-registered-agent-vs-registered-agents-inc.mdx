## What a Registered Agent Actually Does

A registered agent is the person or company designated to receive service of process (lawsuits), government notices and tax correspondence for your LLC. Every state requires one with a physical address in that state, available during business hours. Our guide on [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) covers the basics.

From a CFO's perspective, the risk is rarely the annual fee. The risk is a missed lawsuit. If your agent's address is out of date or your own home address is listed and nobody is there, a court can enter a default judgment before you ever see the complaint. A professional agent exists to prevent exactly that.

Choosing a commercial agent also keeps your home address off the public record, which many owners care about. Our guide to [forming an anonymous LLC](/blog/anonymous-llc-how-to-form-one/) goes further.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Northwest Registered Agent better than Registered Agents Inc?
Neither is better in every case. Northwest tends to win for new LLCs because of its low formation fee and strong support. Registered Agents Inc often wins on standalone agent price. In the Northwest Registered Agent vs Registered Agents Inc decision, match the provider to your situation rather than seeking a universal winner.

### How much does a registered agent cost per year?
Commercial registered agents commonly run from about $100 to $300 per year. Northwest is around $125 and Registered Agents Inc near $100, but confirm current pricing, as it varies by state and over time.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?
Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in that state. You can serve as your own agent, but your address becomes public and you must be reliably available during business hours.

### Can I be my own registered agent?
In most states, yes. The tradeoffs are privacy, availability and the risk of a missed legal notice. Many owners pay a professional to avoid those problems.

### What happens if I don't have a registered agent?
The state can administratively dissolve or suspend your LLC, and you could miss lawsuit notices, leading to default judgments. Reinstating a dissolved LLC can be costly, as covered in our [guide to reinstating a dissolved LLC](/blog/how-to-reinstate-a-dissolved-llc/).

### Can I switch from one registered agent to another?
Yes. You file a change-of-agent form with the state and may pay a small fee. Your new provider usually handles the paperwork. See our [step-by-step change guide](/blog/how-to-change-registered-agent/).

### Does a registered agent make my LLC anonymous?
No. It keeps your personal address off the registered-agent line, but public filings can still list members or managers depending on the state. See our [anonymous LLC guide](/blog/anonymous-llc-how-to-form-one/).

### Is it cheaper to bundle a registered agent with formation?
Often yes for the first year. Services like [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) include a year of agent service on paid plans. Compare the renewal price, since that is where costs usually rise.
