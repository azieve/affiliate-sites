If you are choosing between Stripe Atlas and doola, you are probably a founder who wants a Delaware entity, may not live in the US, and wants someone else to handle the paperwork. The Stripe Atlas vs doola decision comes down to one question: do you want a one-time launch package, or an ongoing compliance partner?

Both services are built for startup founders and international entrepreneurs. Both will form a Delaware entity and get you an EIN. They differ in what happens after the first week. Stripe Atlas is a one-time fee. doola sells a formation package plus optional yearly compliance. If you live in the US and just need a straightforward LLC, a domestic service such as [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) (formation starts at $0 plus state fees, with paid tiers for extras) usually costs far less than either. More on that below.

## What Stripe Atlas Actually Does

Stripe Atlas charges a flat $500 and walks you through an online application. Stripe then files your Delaware incorporation, obtains your EIN, issues founder stock or membership documents, and provides standard legal templates such as 83(b) election guidance for C-corps.

For an LLC, the practical value is speed and the Stripe ecosystem. You get a Stripe account that is ready to accept payments, and Atlas has relationships with partner banks. That matters if your business model depends on Stripe payments from day one.

Where it falls short:

- **It is a launch tool, not a compliance service.** After year one, you are on your own for the Delaware annual tax, registered agent renewal, and federal filings.
- **Its legal templates are built mainly for C-corps.** If you want an LLC operating agreement tailored to your members, you will still need to review it, or hire a lawyer. Our guide to the [LLC operating agreement](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to look for.
- **Support is limited.** Complicated questions about taxes or immigration are outside its scope.

## What doola Actually Does

doola positions itself as a "business-in-a-box" for global founders. After formation, it layers on services such as registered agent, mailing address, compliance reminders, and optional bookkeeping and tax add-ons depending on the plan.

The appeal is that the same company that formed your entity keeps track of deadlines. For a non-US founder who may not know what a Form 5472 is, that has real value. If your LLC is foreign-owned and single-member, you will need to file it every year, and penalties for missing it are steep. See our guide to [Form 5472 for a foreign-owned single-member LLC](/blog/form-5472-for-foreign-owned-single-member-llc/) for the details.

Where it falls short:

- **Cost adds up.** The bundled compliance tiers can cost much more per year than doing the filings yourself.
- **Upsells are common.** Read exactly which tier covers which filing, since some filings are add-ons.
- **Quality of service varies.** Reviews describe quick onboarding but slower turnaround on more complex requests.

## Cost Over Three Years: The Real Comparison

Sticker price is misleading here. Think in terms of three-year cost.

**Stripe Atlas (Delaware LLC), rough picture:**
- Year 1: $500 formation fee
- Delaware LLC annual tax: $300 per year, due June 1 (see our [Delaware LLC franchise tax guide](/blog/delaware-llc-annual-franchise-tax/))
- Registered agent after year one: a renewal fee, so budget around $100 per year (check current terms)
- Tax preparation, if needed: your own accountant

**doola (Delaware LLC), rough picture:**
- Year 1: depends on tier, with the higher-tier packages costing more than Atlas
- Delaware annual tax: $300 per year, whichever provider you use
- Compliance bundle renewals: priced by tier

As a former finance lead would put it, the question is not "which is cheaper in year one?" but "what would I pay a professional to do anyway?" If you will hire a CPA regardless, Atlas's lean model may cost you less. If you would otherwise miss deadlines, doola's reminders may save you penalties.

In my experience, the founders who get burned are the ones who treat formation as the finish line. A Delaware LLC with no US operations still owes the state tax, and a foreign-owned single-member LLC still owes an annual IRS information return. A missed Form 5472 carries a $25,000 penalty under [IRS rules](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-5472), so the compliance layer is worth taking seriously.

## Which One Fits Your Situation?

**Choose Stripe Atlas if:**
- You are building a venture-backed or Stripe-centric company
- You are comfortable handling your own compliance or already have an accountant
- You want a single, known, one-time price

**Choose doola if:**
- You are a non-US founder who wants hands-on guidance
- You want registered agent, address and reminders in one place
- You value having a team to ask about banking, EIN and filings

**Choose neither if:**
- You live in the US and operate in one state. A Delaware entity adds cost without benefit for most small businesses, because you will likely have to register as a foreign LLC in your home state anyway. ## Non-US Founders: Extra Things to Check

If you are outside the US, a few issues apply regardless of provider:

1. **EIN without an SSN.** You can get one, but the process takes longer. See our guide to [getting an EIN without an SSN](/blog/how-to-get-an-ein-without-an-ssn/).
2. **US bank account.** This is often the hardest part. Providers offer introductions, but approval is up to the bank. Our guide to a [US bank account for a foreign-owned LLC](/blog/us-bank-account-for-a-foreign-owned-llc/) covers the options.
3. **Tax in your home country.** A US LLC does not make your income tax-free where you live. Check your local rules and tax treaties with a qualified cross-border tax adviser.
4. **Beneficial ownership reporting.** FinCEN's rules for the Corporate Transparency Act have changed repeatedly, with domestic companies later carved out for some reporting. Check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current requirements and read our [BOI report changes for 2026](/blog/boi-report-changes-and-updates-2026/).

You can confirm Delaware's current filing and tax rules directly with the [Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/).

The Stripe Atlas vs doola question has a simple answer once you know your profile. Pick **Stripe Atlas** if you want a fast, lean launch inside the Stripe ecosystem and you will handle compliance yourself. Pick **doola** if you are a non-US founder who wants help staying compliant after formation. If you are a US resident forming a single-state business, skip both and look at [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) first, with [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) as the runner-up.

Whichever route you take in 2026, put the annual deadlines on a calendar the day your entity is formed. The formation fee is the smallest cost of owning an LLC.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Stripe Atlas better than doola?

Neither is better for everyone. Stripe Atlas suits founders who want a one-time launch package tied to Stripe. doola suits founders who want ongoing compliance help. Compare your total three-year cost, not just the first-year fee.

### How much does Stripe Atlas cost compared to doola?

Stripe Atlas charges a $500 one-time fee. doola's pricing depends on the package and compliance tier you choose. Both are separate from Delaware's $300 annual LLC tax. Confirm current prices on each site.

### Can a non-US resident use Stripe Atlas or doola?

Yes. Both are designed for international founders. You can form a Delaware entity without living in the US, but you will still need to handle US tax filings and possibly local tax obligations at home.

### Do I need a Delaware LLC if I live in the US?

Usually not. If you operate in one state, forming there is often cheaper and simpler. A Delaware LLC typically needs a foreign registration in your home state, which means paying twice.

### What happens if I miss a filing after using Stripe Atlas?

Stripe Atlas does not manage ongoing compliance for you. Missing the Delaware annual tax can cost you penalties and, eventually, good standing. Set reminders or hire a registered agent or accountant who tracks them.

### Does doola file my annual taxes?

Not automatically on every plan. Some tiers include compliance reminders or filings, while tax preparation may be an add-on. Check what your tier covers before you buy.

### What is the best Stripe Atlas alternative for US residents?

For most US small businesses, ZenBusiness is the best value, with LegalZoom as a secondary option. See our [Stripe Atlas review](/blog/stripe-atlas-review/) for more alternatives.

### Do I still need a registered agent after the first year?

Yes. Every LLC must maintain a registered agent. Learn more in [What Is a Registered Agent?](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/)
