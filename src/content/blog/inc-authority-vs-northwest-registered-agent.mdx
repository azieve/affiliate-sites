## Inc Authority vs Northwest Registered Agent: The Short Version

Here is the verdict up front, then the detail.

- **[Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/)** advertises $0 formation service fees on its entry-level package (you still pay your state's filing fee). It makes its money on upgrades, add-ons, and recurring services. It suits cost-conscious founders who know exactly what they need and will say no to upsells.
- **[Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/)** charges a flat formation fee (historically $39 plus state fees) and includes a year of registered agent service. It is built around privacy and human support. It suits anyone who does not want their home address in public records.

In my experience, the question that decides this is not price. It is whether you care that your name and address are discoverable through the state's business search. If you do, Northwest has a clear edge. If you do not, Inc Authority's lower upfront cost is hard to ignore.

## Registered Agent Service: Who Handles It Better?

A registered agent receives lawsuits, government notices, and tax correspondence on your LLC's behalf. Missing one of these can lead to a default judgment or administrative dissolution. Our guide on [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) covers the basics.

**Northwest** treats registered agent service as its core product. Its local offices, same-day scanning of documents, and consistent pricing are the reason many attorneys recommend it. It also offers service in all 50 states.

**Inc Authority** offers registered agent service, but it is more of an add-on to the formation package than the headline feature. That can work fine for a small, low-risk LLC. It is worth confirming how notices are delivered to you and how quickly.

As a rule of thumb, if you expect to be sued, serve many customers, or operate in more than one state, favor the provider with the stronger registered agent operation. In a multi-state scenario, see our guide to [multi-state LLC services](/blog/best-llc-service-for-multi-state-business/).

## Privacy: The Biggest Real-World Difference

Most states publish LLC filings in a searchable database. Without a privacy-focused setup, your name, home address, and sometimes your email can be exposed there. Marketers, data brokers, and in rare cases, bad actors scrape those records.

Northwest's default of using its own address is the strongest privacy feature in this matchup. Inc Authority can still be used for a private LLC, but you may need to purchase the right add-on or choose a state with strong privacy protections, such as New Mexico, where members and managers are not required to be listed on the Articles of Organization. For help picking a state, see our [best state to form an LLC](/blog/best-state-to-form-an-llc/) comparison.

One regulatory note for 2026: the Corporate Transparency Act's beneficial ownership reporting rules were substantially narrowed in 2025. FinCEN's [interim final rule](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) exempted U.S.-formed companies and their beneficial owners from BOI reporting. Rules in this area have changed repeatedly, so confirm current requirements on FinCEN's site or with a professional. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) tracks the latest.

## Hidden Costs and Gotchas to Watch For in 2026

Whichever provider you choose, budget for these items.

1. **State filing fees.** These are paid to the state regardless of the service you use. See the [IRS guidance on LLCs](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc) for the federal tax side.
2. **Annual reports and franchise taxes.** Delaware charges a $300 annual LLC tax. California charges an $800 minimum franchise tax. Texas has a franchise tax threshold. No formation service pays these for you.
3. **Registered agent renewals.** The intro price is rarely the ongoing price.
4. **Optional add-ons.** EIN service, expedited filing, and compliance monitoring can add $50 to $200 or more.
5. **Rush processing.** Faster filing can carry state expedite fees plus service fees.

A concrete scenario: a freelancer in Texas forms an LLC through a $0 package. State fee is $300. They then add an EIN service for roughly $70 and a registered agent for around $100 per year. Their "free" LLC costs about $470 in year one. The same founder with Northwest pays about $39 plus $300, and registered agent service is covered for year one, for a total of about $339. Your numbers will vary, but the lesson holds: add up the all-in cost, not the headline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Inc Authority really free?

Inc Authority advertises $0 in service fees on its basic formation package, but you still pay your state's filing fee. Optional services and upgrades cost extra, so total cost depends on what you add. Check the current plan page before ordering.

### How much does Northwest Registered Agent cost?

Northwest has historically charged $39 for formation plus state fees, with the first year of registered agent service included. Renewal has been around $125 per year. Pricing can change, so verify on the provider's site.

### Which is better for privacy, Inc Authority or Northwest?

Northwest is generally stronger for privacy because it uses its own address on public filings by default. With Inc Authority you may need extra add-ons or a privacy-friendly state to get similar protection.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in the state of formation. You can serve as your own agent, but that puts your address on public record and requires you to be available during business hours.

### Can I switch registered agents later?

Yes. You file a change of registered agent form with your state, and some states charge a small fee. See our guide on [how to change your registered agent](/blog/how-to-change-registered-agent/) for the steps.

### Is it cheaper to form an LLC myself?

Filing directly with your state costs only the state fee, but you handle the paperwork, EIN, operating agreement, and compliance. Services save time and reduce errors. See our guide on the [cheapest way to form an LLC](/blog/cheapest-way-to-form-an-llc/) for strategies.

### Does either service include an operating agreement?

Northwest includes an operating agreement template. Inc Authority's inclusion varies by plan. Either way, a generic template may not fit multi-member LLCs, so consider legal review. Our [operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to include.

### Do I still need to file a BOI report in 2026?

As of the 2025 FinCEN interim final rule, domestic U.S. companies are generally exempt from BOI reporting. The rules have shifted several times, so check FinCEN's site or ask a professional before relying on this.
