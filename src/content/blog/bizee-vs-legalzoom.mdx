One built its reputation on a genuinely free filing tier; the other has spent decades buying brand recognition through TV commercials and legal services marketing. But name recognition and value aren't the same thing — and in 2026, neither service is the obvious best choice for most business owners.

This comparison breaks down exactly what you get with each service, what you'll actually pay when you factor in add-ons and renewals, and where a third option — [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/), starting at $0 plus state fees — quietly outperforms both on the features that matter most for long-term compliance. If you want the short answer: ZenBusiness handles the most common use case better than either competitor at a comparable price. But the full picture is worth understanding before you file.

## Pricing Breakdown: What You Actually Pay

This is where the Bizee vs LegalZoom comparison gets complicated — and where most people end up surprised by their total annual cost.

### Bizee Pricing in 2026

Bizee offers three tiers:

- **Free (Basic):** $0 service fee + state filing fee. Includes articles of organization preparation and filing, registered agent service (free for year one, then $119/year), and electronic processing.
- **Gold:** $149 + state filing fee. Adds an operating agreement template, EIN (Tax ID) filing assistance, a business banking resolution document, and a physical company kit.
- **Platinum:** $299 + state filing fee. Adds business contract templates, a business domain and website, and priority customer support.

The catch most people miss: registered agent service renews at $119/year after year one. That's reasonable — but it's a cost that doesn't show up on the "Free" plan checkout screen. In states with high filing fees (California charges $70, New York charges $200, Massachusetts charges $500), your actual out-of-pocket cost for a supposedly free formation can exceed $600 in year one and $119+ every year after.

For a full breakdown of what you'll spend by state, our guide to [how much it costs to form an LLC](/learn/how-much-does-an-llc-cost/) covers this in detail.

### LegalZoom Pricing in 2026

LegalZoom has shifted toward a subscription-based model:

- **Basic:** $0 + state filing fee. Extremely minimal — document preparation only, with very limited support.
- **Pro:** ~$249/year. Includes registered agent service, an operating agreement, and limited attorney consultation access.
- **Premium:** ~$299/year. Adds expanded attorney access for covered legal matters, business license research, and tax advisory sessions.

LegalZoom's registered agent service as a standalone product runs approximately $249/year — roughly double what Bizee charges for the same service in renewal years. If you're comparing true apples-to-apples (LLC formation plus year one of registered agent service), [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/)'s Pro subscription is price-competitive with Bizee's Gold tier but layers in attorney access that Bizee doesn't offer.

### The Hidden Upsells Both Services Use

Both Bizee and LegalZoom aggressively upsell during checkout. Common additions include:

- Expedited filing ($50–$100 added fee)
- Annual report filing assistance ($100–$150/year, though most states let you file this yourself for free or a small direct fee)
- Business license compliance packages
- Compliance alert services
- Bank account setup referrals

In my experience working with small business clients, the biggest sticker shock almost always comes at the first annual renewal — not at formation. A founder who paid $0 in year one with Bizee often can't understand why year-two costs $119 for registered agent service plus whatever compliance add-ons they agreed to at checkout. Read renewal terms carefully before you file.

## Registered Agent Services: A Key Annual Cost

Every LLC must maintain a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) — a designated individual or service with a physical address in your state of formation, responsible for receiving legal notices and official correspondence. This isn't optional, and it's a recurring annual expense.

Here's how the major services compare on registered agent pricing:

- **Bizee:** Free for year one, then $119/year
- **LegalZoom:** Included in Pro/Premium subscription. Standalone: ~$249/year
- **[ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/):** Included in Pro plan ($199/year total — formation plus registered agent bundled)
- **[Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/):** $125/year standalone, with a strong privacy-focused reputation and highly responsive service
- **[Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/):** Free for year one, then approximately $199/year

For long-term annual costs, Bizee's $119/year registered agent renewal is competitive. LegalZoom's $249/year standalone rate is harder to justify against alternatives, though it disappears if you're already paying for the subscription for attorney access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Bizee really free to form an LLC?**

Bizee's service fee is $0 on the Basic plan, but you still pay your state's filing fee — which ranges from $40 (Kentucky) to $500 (Massachusetts). Registered agent service is also free only for year one, then renews at $119/year. "Free" refers to Bizee's service fee, not your total out-of-pocket cost.

**How does LegalZoom's subscription model work?**

LegalZoom's Pro (~$249/year) and Premium (~$299/year) plans are annual subscriptions that include registered agent service and operating agreement access. If you cancel, you'll need to arrange registered agent service separately. The model makes sense if you'll actively use the attorney access and legal document features; it's expensive if you only need a one-time LLC filing.

**Is Bizee the same as Incfile?**

Yes. Bizee rebranded from Incfile in 2023. The pricing structure, plan tiers, registered agent service, and filing process are largely the same. Older reviews referencing "Incfile" describe the same company and roughly the same product.

**Which service is faster for LLC formation?**

Bizee and ZenBusiness tend to process faster than LegalZoom on standard filings. However, actual turnaround time depends primarily on your state's processing speed, which ranges from same-day (Wyoming) to several weeks (New York, California). All services offer expedited options for a fee, but state processing time is the main variable.

**Do I need a registered agent service, or can I be my own registered agent?**

You can serve as your own registered agent in most states — as long as you have a physical address in that state and are available during business hours. However, using a service protects your privacy (your agent's address appears on public records instead of yours) and ensures you won't miss time-sensitive legal notices. Our guide explains [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) and what happens if your LLC goes without one.

**Does Bizee's free plan include an operating agreement?**

No. Operating agreement templates are only included in Bizee's Gold plan ($149 + state fee) and above. For multi-member LLCs, an operating agreement is legally required in some states and practically essential in all of them. Single-member LLCs should also have one on file to reinforce the separation between personal and business assets.

**What is an EIN and do I need one for my LLC?**

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is a federal tax ID issued by the [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers) that your LLC uses for tax filing, hiring employees, and opening a business bank account. You need one if you have employees, operate a multi-member LLC, or plan to open a business checking account (most banks require it). Both Bizee (Gold+) and LegalZoom (Pro+) include EIN filing assistance in paid tiers. You can also obtain an EIN directly through the IRS website for free — the process takes about 15 minutes online.

**Can I switch my registered agent after forming with Bizee or LegalZoom?**

Yes, and it's straightforward. Once your LLC is formed, you can change registered agents at any time by filing a simple form with your state and paying a small fee (typically $25–$50). If you started with Bizee's free tier and want to move your registered agent to a service with better privacy features or pricing, the switch doesn't affect your LLC's legal standing.
