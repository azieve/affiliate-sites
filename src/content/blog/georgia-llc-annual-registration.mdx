## What Is Georgia LLC Annual Registration?

Georgia calls it an "annual registration." In most other states it would be an annual report. It is a yearly filing that confirms your LLC's current information on the public record. It does not report revenue, profit or taxes.

Each year you confirm or update:

- The LLC's legal name and principal office address
- The registered agent's name and Georgia street address
- The names and addresses of at least one organizer, member or manager (officer information)
- The LLC's NAICS/business type details, if requested on the form

Think of it as a change-of-address form for the state's records. If your details haven't changed, you are mostly confirming what is already on file.

## Georgia Annual Registration Deadline and Fee for 2026

Here are the numbers that matter:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Filing window | January 1 through April 1 each year |
| First filing due | The calendar year after your LLC was formed |
| Filing fee | $50 (online through the Secretary of State's eCorp portal) |
| Late penalty | $25 if filed after April 1 |
| Filing method | Online is fastest; paper filings are slower |

Two points cause most of the confusion.

**The first filing isn't due in your formation year.** If your LLC was formed in 2026, your first annual registration is due between January 1 and April 1, 2027. An LLC formed in 2025 has its 2026 registration due in the same January–April window this year.

**The window is the same for every LLC.** It doesn't depend on your formation date, unlike states such as Florida or Wyoming, where the due date follows your formation anniversary or is set by a fixed date. You don't have to track an anniversary, only the April 1 cutoff.

## How to File Your Georgia LLC Annual Registration Step by Step

The whole process happens through the Secretary of State's eCorp system.

1. **Go to the eCorp portal.** Start from the [Georgia Secretary of State's website](https://sos.ga.gov/) and choose the business filings section. Search for your LLC by name or control number.
2. **Select "Annual Registration."** Pick your entity, then choose the filing for the current year.
3. **Review your information.** Check the principal office address, registered agent and officer details. Correct anything that is out of date. Changing the registered agent on the annual form is allowed, but the new agent must have consented.
4. **Confirm the business details.** Answer any questions about your business type and mailing address.
5. **Pay the $50 fee.** Use a credit or debit card. The portal issues a confirmation.
6. **Save your receipt and the filed registration.** Download the PDF and keep it with your corporate records.

Don't wait for a reminder to arrive. Georgia sends courtesy emails only if the address on file is current, and many owners have never updated it since formation.

## What Happens If You Miss the Georgia Annual Registration Deadline?

Missing April 1 doesn't dissolve your LLC overnight, but the consequences escalate.

- **Late penalty.** File after April 1 and you owe an additional $25 on top of the $50 fee.
- **Notice from the state.** If no registration is filed, the Secretary of State can begin the administrative dissolution process and send notice to your registered agent or address of record.
- **Administrative dissolution.** If you don't cure the problem within the period given in the notice, the state can dissolve your LLC. A dissolved LLC loses good standing and may be unable to sign contracts, open bank accounts or get a good standing certificate.
- **Liability questions.** Operating after dissolution creates legal ambiguity. Courts generally protect owners who reinstate promptly, but you shouldn't test that. Ask an attorney about your specific situation.

## How to Reinstate a Georgia LLC After Administrative Dissolution

If the state has already dissolved your LLC, reinstatement is usually possible. In general you will need to:

1. File the missing annual registration(s) through eCorp.
2. Pay the filing fees plus any applicable penalties.
3. Make sure your registered agent and address are current.
4. Confirm the LLC's name is still available if the state requires it.

Once accepted, your LLC is restored and generally treated as though it had continued in existence, but the exact effect depends on the circumstances. ## Practical Tips to Never Miss Georgia LLC Annual Registration

As a former CFO-style checklist person, I recommend three habits:

1. **Set two calendar reminders.** One on January 2 and one on March 1. The first is the day the window opens. The second gives you a buffer before April 1.
2. **Update your email on file.** Use a shared business inbox rather than a personal address that could go stale.
3. **File early in the window.** There is no advantage to waiting. The fee is the same on January 2 as on March 31.

Bundle it with other January tasks such as reviewing insurance, updating your operating agreement and gathering documents for your tax preparer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is the Georgia LLC annual registration fee?

The fee is $50 when filed online through the Secretary of State's eCorp system. A $25 late penalty applies if you file after April 1. Confirm current amounts with the Secretary of State before filing.

### When is the Georgia LLC annual registration due?

Between January 1 and April 1 each year. Your first filing is due in the calendar year after the LLC was formed.

### Do I need to file an annual registration in the year I form my LLC?

No. If you form your LLC in 2026, your first annual registration is due January 1 to April 1, 2027.

### What happens if I miss the Georgia annual registration deadline?

You owe a $25 late penalty, and if the registration remains unfiled, the state can begin administrative dissolution proceedings. Filing promptly usually prevents dissolution.

### Is the Georgia annual registration the same as a BOI report?

No. The annual registration is a state filing with the Georgia Secretary of State. The BOI report is a separate federal requirement administered by FinCEN, and its rules have changed. See our [BOI vs annual report comparison](/blog/boi-report-vs-annual-report-what-is-the-difference/).

### Can I change my registered agent on my annual registration?

Generally yes, provided the new agent has agreed to serve and has a Georgia street address. You can also file a separate change form at any time.

### Can a service file my Georgia LLC annual registration for me?

Yes. Formation and compliance providers such as [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) and [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) offer reminders or filing assistance, sometimes as paid add-ons. You can also file yourself for the state fee.

### Does Georgia charge an annual franchise tax on LLCs?

Georgia doesn't charge a flat annual LLC fee like Delaware or California. The annual registration fee is the main recurring state filing cost, though your LLC may owe income taxes and other business taxes depending on its activity. Consult a Georgia tax professional.
