## What Is LLC Attorney?

LLC Attorney is an online LLC formation service founded with the premise that business formation should involve licensed attorneys, not just automated filing systems. The company pairs you with a legal team that reviews your documents and oversees the formation process — a model that contrasts with purely software-driven competitors.

This matters more than it sounds. In 2026, the regulatory environment for small businesses has grown increasingly complex. Between the ongoing [BOI reporting requirements](/blog/boi-report-for-llc-owners-2026/) under the Corporate Transparency Act, state-by-state compliance variations, and the IRS's expanding scrutiny of single-member LLCs, having a lawyer in your corner during formation can catch problems before they become expensive.

LLC Attorney handles all 50 states and offers services ranging from basic LLC filing to full business setup packages that include EIN registration, operating agreements, and compliance calendars.

## LLC Attorney Pricing and Plans (2026)

Understanding LLC Attorney's pricing requires reading carefully — like most formation services, the headline number doesn't tell the full story.

**Basic Plan — Starting around $0 + state fees**
LLC Attorney offers a free formation tier for simple filings. You handle more of the process yourself, and the attorney oversight is limited. State filing fees still apply and vary widely (Delaware is $90; California is $70; Wyoming is just $100). This tier is fine if your only goal is getting the documents filed.

**Standard Plan — Approximately $149 + state fees**
This is where the attorney involvement becomes meaningful. You get a reviewed operating agreement, EIN filing, registered agent service for one year, and a compliance reminder system. For most single-member LLCs, this is the sweet spot.

**Premium Plan — Approximately $299 + state fees**
The premium tier adds personalized attorney consultations (typically 30-60 minutes), priority processing, and an expanded package of legal templates — things like member certificates, meeting minutes templates, and banking resolution letters.

**Registered Agent Add-On — $99–$149/year**
Like most competitors, LLC Attorney upsells registered agent service after the first year. Their rate sits in the mid-range compared to [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/), which charges $125/year and includes mail forwarding, and [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/), whose registered agent bundles into their $199/year Starter plan.

One area where LLC Attorney differentiates: the operating agreement they produce is genuinely attorney-drafted, not just a fill-in-the-blank template. I've reviewed enough generic operating agreements from budget services to know this distinction matters — especially if you're forming a multi-member LLC or plan to raise outside capital.

## LLC Attorney Features: What You Actually Get

Here's what's included across their plans, with honest notes on where the value shows up:

**Attorney-Reviewed Documents**
This is the core differentiator. Unlike [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) (which uses template-generated documents) or even [Northwest Registered Agent](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/), LLC Attorney has a licensed attorney sign off on your operating agreement and articles of organization before they're filed. In my experience, this catches edge cases that automated systems miss — particularly for LLCs with unusual ownership structures or operating in regulated industries.

**EIN Registration**
Included in the Standard and Premium tiers. The IRS EIN application is free to file yourself, but having the formation service handle it saves 30-60 minutes of confusion and guarantees the EIN is linked correctly to your LLC's legal name.

**Operating Agreement**
Every LLC should have one, even in states that don't require it. LLC Attorney's Standard and Premium plans include a custom operating agreement — not just a state-specific template, but a document tailored to your LLC's structure. This is particularly important for [multi-member LLCs](/blog/llc-member-vs-manager-managed/) where profit allocation, decision-making authority, and exit provisions need to be clearly defined.

**BOI Filing Assistance**
As of 2026, most new LLCs must file a Beneficial Ownership Information report with FinCEN within 90 days of formation. LLC Attorney's premium plans include guidance on this filing, which is a legitimate value-add given how many founders still don't know this requirement exists. For a deeper dive, see our [BOI report guide](/blog/boi-report-for-llc-owners-2026/).

**Compliance Calendar and Alerts**
Every plan includes reminders for annual report deadlines, registered agent renewal, and state fee due dates. Simple feature, but practically valuable — missing an annual report filing can trigger penalties or even administrative dissolution of your LLC.

**Business Banking Guidance**
LLC Attorney offers guidance on opening a business bank account, including documentation checklists. They don't have partnerships with specific banks the way some competitors do, which is both a pro (no conflict of interest) and a con (no streamlined integration with Mercury or Relay).

## Who Should Actually Use LLC Attorney?

LLC Attorney is the right fit for:

**Multi-member LLCs.** If you have co-founders or investors, an attorney-reviewed operating agreement is worth the extra cost. Disputes over ownership percentages and decision-making authority are among the most common — and expensive — small business legal problems. Starting with a properly drafted document reduces that risk.

**LLCs in regulated industries.** Healthcare, financial services, real estate investing, and certain professional services have state-specific requirements for their formation documents. An attorney who understands your industry can flag compliance issues that a software template won't catch.

**Founders who want attorney access without hiring a full-service law firm.** A traditional business attorney can charge $500–$2,000+ to form an LLC from scratch. LLC Attorney's premium tier at ~$299 gives you professional legal review at a fraction of that cost.

**Founders unsure about their state choice.** If you're weighing [which state to form your LLC in](/blog/best-state-to-form-an-llc/), an attorney consultation can help you make the right decision based on your specific business model — not just what you read in a generic guide.

LLC Attorney is probably **not** the best fit for:

- Straightforward single-member LLCs where speed and price are the only factors
- Founders who've already formed LLCs and know the process
- Budget-constrained founders where every dollar matters (ZenBusiness or Northwest are better here)
