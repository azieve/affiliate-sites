## What Does a Registered Agent Actually Do?

Before comparing prices, it's worth understanding what you're actually paying for — because "registered agent service" can mean different things at different price points.

A registered agent, sometimes called a statutory agent or resident agent, is a designated point of contact for your LLC. According to the [IRS and individual state statutes](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/llcs), every LLC must maintain a registered agent with a physical street address in the state of formation. P.O. boxes don't count.

The registered agent's core job is to receive:

- **Service of process** — legal documents like lawsuits, subpoenas, and court orders
- **State compliance notices** — annual report reminders, tax notices, and regulatory filings
- **Secretary of State correspondence** — official state government mail

The agent must be available at their address during normal business hours, Monday through Friday. That sounds simple, but the implications matter. If your LLC gets sued and no one is at the registered agent address to receive the summons, you could miss your response deadline and have a default judgment entered against you — without ever knowing the lawsuit existed.

## How Much Should You Pay for a Registered Agent?

### 5. DIY (Acting as Your Own Registered Agent) — $0/year

In most states, you can serve as your own registered agent if you have a physical street address in the state and are available during business hours. The [U.S. Small Business Administration](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business) confirms this is a legal option for most entities.

The $0 price tag is appealing, but there are real operational trade-offs:

- **Your home address becomes public record.** State filings are public documents. If you list your home address as your registered agent address, it's visible to anyone who searches your LLC's state registration.
- **You must be available at that address during all business hours, every business day.** If you travel, work remotely from multiple locations, or ever work from a coffee shop, you could technically be out of compliance.
- **Service of process gets delivered to you personally.** If your business is ever sued, a process server shows up at your home or office — in front of employees, neighbors, or family.

For a solo freelancer operating from a dedicated home office who never travels and values privacy less, DIY can work. For most businesses, the privacy and professionalism argument alone makes $125/year for Northwest a worthwhile spend.

## Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

Finding the cheapest registered agent service isn't just about the headline price. Watch for these common hidden costs that can inflate your actual spend:

**Auto-renewal without reminder:** Several services auto-renew annually without a prominent reminder email. If you've switched services but forget to cancel, you can end up paying two registered agents for the same year.

**Multi-state fees:** If your LLC operates in multiple states, you'll need a registered agent in each state where you're registered as a foreign LLC. Prices multiply quickly. Northwest's multi-state pricing remains competitive, but always confirm per-state rates before assuming the headline price applies everywhere.

**Document forwarding fees:** Some budget providers charge per-document forwarding fees beyond a certain volume. For most small businesses this never triggers, but if you're in an industry with heavy government correspondence, it can add up.

**"Compliance package" upsells:** Services like ZenBusiness and Bizee aggressively offer compliance add-ons during checkout. Annual report filing, operating agreement drafts, and EIN registration can be useful — but they're separate line items, not part of registered agent service itself.

## FAQ: Cheapest Registered Agent Service

### How much does a registered agent service cost?

Registered agent services in 2026 range from $0 (bundled free first year with LLC formation) to $299+/year for premium services. The practical budget range for a quality paid service is $119–$125/year. Northwest Registered Agent at $125/year and Bizee at $119/year are the two lowest-cost paid options with full professional-grade service.

### Can I be my own registered agent to save money?

Yes, in most states you can act as your own registered agent if you have a physical street address in the state and are available during normal business hours. The main drawbacks are that your home address becomes public record, you must be present at that address every business day, and legal papers get served directly to you personally. For most business owners, paying $100–$125/year for privacy and reliability is worth it.

### What is the cheapest registered agent service with no hidden fees?

Northwest Registered Agent is widely considered the most transparent option in terms of pricing. Their $125/year fee covers the full service with no document-forwarding surcharges, no auto-renew tricks, and no aggressive upsell campaigns. Bizee at $119/year is slightly cheaper but does use more aggressive upsell prompts during checkout and renewal.

### Do I need a registered agent in every state?

You need a registered agent in every state where your LLC is formally registered. If you've only formed your LLC in one state, you only need one registered agent. If you've registered as a foreign LLC in additional states — for example, because you have employees or a physical location there — you need a registered agent in each of those states too.

### What happens if I don't have a registered agent?

Operating without a registered agent puts your LLC out of compliance with your state's LLC statute. Consequences range from administrative dissolution of your LLC (your state literally closes it) to missing critical legal notices like lawsuits. If a default judgment is entered because you never received the service of process, it can be extremely difficult and expensive to undo. This is not a corner worth cutting.

### Is free registered agent service actually free?

"Free" registered agent service typically means the cost is bundled into an LLC formation package — it's free in the first year, after which annual renewal fees apply. Services like Bizee (free first year, then $119/year) and ZenBusiness (free first year, then $199/year) follow this model. Genuinely free registered agent service in perpetuity generally means you're acting as your own registered agent, which has its own trade-offs.

### Can I switch registered agents after my LLC is formed?

Yes. Changing your registered agent requires filing a Statement of Change of Registered Agent (or equivalent form) with your state's Secretary of State office, along with a small filing fee (typically $10–$50). Most professional registered agent services will handle this paperwork for you when you sign up. It's a routine process and there's no meaningful penalty for switching.

Their service includes same-day document scanning, a privacy-forward approach that doesn't sell your data, and actual knowledgeable support staff.
