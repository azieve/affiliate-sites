If you are weighing Inc Authority vs ZenBusiness, you are probably trying to avoid paying a premium for something you could do yourself, while still getting your LLC filed correctly and kept in good standing. That is a sensible goal. Both companies will file your Articles of Organization for you, but they differ in how they price the work, what they bundle, and how much they lean on upsells after checkout.

Our short answer: [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) is the better fit for most new owners in 2026. It offers a $0 service-fee starter tier (plus your state filing fee) and clear paid tiers at roughly $199 and $349 per year, with compliance reminders built in. [Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/) is worth a look if you want the lowest possible upfront cost and are comfortable declining add-ons. The details below should help you decide which is right for you.

## What Each Service Actually Does

Both services submit your formation paperwork to the Secretary of State (or equivalent agency) and charge the state's filing fee on top of their own. Before you pay anyone, read how the IRS describes [LLC classification and taxation](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc). A formation service does not change how your LLC is taxed. It only handles paperwork.

In my experience, the most common mistake I see is owners believing a formation service replaces ongoing compliance. It does not. Filing is a one-time event. Annual reports, registered agent maintenance, tax elections and keeping personal and business money separate are what keep your liability protection intact.

## ZenBusiness Plans and Pricing in 2026

ZenBusiness structures its offering in three tiers. Exact contents shift, so verify at checkout.

- **Starter ($0 + state fee):** Files your Articles of Organization. It is a bare-bones option, and you should expect to handle registered agent service and several other items yourself or pay extra.
- **Pro (about $199/year):** Typically adds a registered agent, an operating agreement template, EIN assistance and compliance reminders. This is the tier most people actually need.
- **Premium (about $349/year):** Adds extras such as faster processing and additional business tools. Useful for some, unnecessary for many.

The $0 starter price is a hook, and I would not treat it as your real cost. A new LLC needs a registered agent in the state of formation. If your starter plan does not include one, you will pay for it separately or serve as your own agent, which publishes your home address. Our deeper [ZenBusiness review](/reviews/zenbusiness/) breaks down each tier line by line.

**Where ZenBusiness is strong:** guided onboarding, clear compliance reminders and a straightforward dashboard. Where it is weaker: the upsell prompts after checkout can be persistent, and renewal pricing deserves a close look.

## Inc Authority Plans and Pricing in 2026

## Feature-by-Feature Comparison

### Formation filing and speed

Both services submit your filing to the state. Processing speed is mostly controlled by the state agency, not by the formation company. Paid expedite options exist with some providers, and a few states process online filings within a day or two while others take weeks. Check your state's current processing time before paying for a rush upgrade.

### Registered agent

Every LLC needs one. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) generally includes a registered agent for the first year at the Pro level, then renews at a recurring price. With [Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/), coverage depends on your package. If privacy is your top concern, a dedicated agent provider may suit you better. See our guide to [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) for what the role involves.

### Operating agreement

An operating agreement is not filed with the state in most places, but it matters. It documents how the business is run and supports your separation from the company if a creditor ever challenges your limited liability status. Both services offer templates at certain tiers. Our [LLC operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to include. Multi-member LLCs in particular should have an attorney review the agreement.

### EIN

You can get an EIN directly from the IRS at no cost. If a service charges for it, you are paying for convenience. This is one of the easiest places to save money.

### Ongoing compliance

This is where ZenBusiness tends to pull ahead for beginners, since reminders and guided annual report filing come with its higher tiers. With Inc Authority, you will want to confirm exactly which reminders and filings are included before relying on it.

### Beneficial ownership reporting

Federal rules on Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting have changed substantially. As of March 2025, FinCEN removed the reporting requirement for U.S.-formed companies and their owners, but rules can shift again. Check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status, and see our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) for context. Do not pay a formation service extra for a filing you may not need.

Take a single-member LLC in a state with a $100 filing fee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Inc Authority cheaper than ZenBusiness?

Inc Authority often has the lower headline price. After you add a registered agent, an operating agreement and other items, the total can be similar to ZenBusiness's Pro tier. Compare first-year totals, not entry prices.

### Is ZenBusiness worth it for a new LLC?

For many first-time owners, yes. The Pro tier bundles a registered agent, templates and compliance reminders. If you are comfortable doing those yourself, you can file directly with your state for just the filing fee.

### Do I need a formation service to start an LLC?

No. You can file directly with your Secretary of State. A service saves time and handles paperwork, but it does not change your legal protections or tax treatment.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in the state. You can serve as your own, hire a commercial agent or use one bundled with a formation plan.

### What happens if I miss my LLC annual report?

Consequences vary by state, from late fees to administrative dissolution. Reinstatement can cost more than the original filing, which is why reminder tools matter.

### Can I get an EIN for free?

Yes. The IRS issues EINs at no charge. Any fee from a formation service is for convenience.

### Which is better for privacy, Inc Authority or ZenBusiness?

Neither is primarily a privacy product. If privacy is your main concern, a dedicated registered agent provider such as [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) may be a better fit.

### Can I switch services after I form my LLC?

Yes. You can change your registered agent and move compliance tasks elsewhere without re-forming the company. Check each provider's cancellation and renewal terms first.
