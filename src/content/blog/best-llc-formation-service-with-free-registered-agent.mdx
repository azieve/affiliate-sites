What is negotiable is whether you pay extra for it. Several LLC formation services bundle registered agent service into their base plans, giving you professional coverage at no additional charge (at least for the first year). If you're comparing formation services in 2026, the free registered agent offer is one of the highest-value differences between providers.

## Why Free Registered Agent Service Matters

Before diving into comparisons, it's worth quantifying what you're actually getting.

Professional registered agent services typically cost **$100–$300 per year** when purchased separately. Over the first two or three years of your LLC, that's a meaningful line item in your operating budget — especially for solopreneurs and early-stage businesses watching every dollar.

When an LLC formation service includes a free registered agent, they're effectively subsidizing that cost in exchange for your formation fee and the hope that you'll stay on as a long-term customer. That's a fair trade, as long as you understand the renewal pricing upfront.

The critical question isn't just "is it free?" — it's "how long is it free, what happens at renewal, and is that renewal price reasonable?"

According to the IRS Small Business and Self-Employed Tax Center, every single LLC must designate a registered agent with a physical address in the state of formation. Virtual offices and PO Boxes don't qualify in most states. Using a professional service ensures compliance while keeping your personal home address off public records — a benefit that goes well beyond simple convenience.

## What to Look for Beyond "Free"

The free registered agent headline grabs attention, but there are several other factors that determine whether a formation service is truly the best choice for your situation.

### Privacy Practices

Your registered agent's address appears on public state records. If you use a service that lists your personal address — or their own generic address that's shared by thousands of LLCs — you're either exposed or lost in the noise. Northwest's use of their own address on formation documents is one of the industry's best privacy practices.

### Document Handling

The core function of a registered agent is accepting legal documents on your behalf. When a lawsuit is filed against your LLC, the process server goes to your registered agent's address. How quickly does the agent scan and forward those documents? Northwest's digital document delivery is fast and reliable. Other services have had complaints about delays.

### Annual Report Reminders

Most states require annual or biennial reports with a filing fee. Miss the deadline and you risk late fees, penalties, or even administrative dissolution. Good registered agent services proactively remind you of upcoming deadlines with enough lead time to act. This is a small but genuinely valuable operational benefit.

### Scalability

If you plan to register in multiple states — a common strategy for Amazon FBA sellers, real estate investors, and trucking companies — you'll want a registered agent service that can cover all states at predictable pricing. Northwest offers multi-state coverage at $125/state/year, which is transparent and scalable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do all LLC formation services include free registered agent service?

No. Some services like LegalZoom only include registered agent on paid annual plans. Others like Northwest, ZenBusiness, and Bizee include one year free with even their base formation plans. Always check whether the free period applies to the plan you're choosing.

### How long is registered agent service free with these services?

Typically one year from the date of formation. ZenBusiness includes it ongoing on their Pro and Premium plans. After the free period expires, you'll either be auto-enrolled in a paid plan or need to add it manually depending on the service.

### Can I be my own registered agent instead of using a service?

Yes, in most states you can serve as your own registered agent if you have a physical address in the state and are available during business hours. However, this exposes your home or office address publicly and means you must be present to receive legal documents — a significant inconvenience for LLC owners who travel or work remotely.

### What happens after the free registered agent year ends?

The service will typically notify you before renewal. You can choose to continue with that service, switch to another, or designate yourself. Switching registered agents requires filing a change of registered agent form with your state, which usually costs $10–$50.

### Is it worth paying for a registered agent service instead of listing my own address?

For most LLC owners, yes. A registered agent service keeps your personal address off public records, ensures reliable document handling, and provides annual report reminders. The $100–$150/year cost is modest compared to the privacy and compliance benefits.

### Which state has the cheapest registered agent requirements?

Wyoming, Delaware, and New Mexico are often cited as LLC-friendly states with low overall costs. Wyoming has no state income tax on LLCs and low registered agent fees. However, if you do business primarily in your home state, forming in another state means you'll need to register as a foreign LLC there anyway — often negating the cost savings.

### Can I switch registered agents without dissolving my LLC?

Yes. Changing your registered agent is a routine administrative action. You file a Statement of Change of Registered Agent with your state, pay the filing fee (usually $10–$50), and the change takes effect. Most registered agent services will assist you with this process.

### Does the registered agent need to be in the same state as my LLC?

Yes. Your registered agent must have a physical street address (not a PO Box) in the state where your LLC is registered. If you form in Delaware, your Delaware registered agent must have a Delaware address. Services like Northwest maintain office addresses in all 50 states to accommodate this.
