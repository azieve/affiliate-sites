# LegalZoom vs BetterLegal (2026): Which LLC Formation Service Is Better?

If you are stuck between a household name and a newer, leaner competitor, the **LegalZoom vs BetterLegal** decision comes down to three things: what you actually pay over the first two years, how quickly your LLC is filed, and how much help you get once the paperwork is done. This guide walks through each, using what I have seen after years of testing formation services.

Short version: LegalZoom is the established, feature-rich option with the most add-ons and the highest upsell pressure. BetterLegal is the newer, simpler option that tends to appeal to founders who want a fast, low-friction filing. If neither feels like the right fit, [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) is the service I most often recommend as a middle path. It has a $0 starter tier (plus state fees) and bundled tiers around $199 and $349 per year, with a clear feature ladder and no long list of surprise add-ons.

## Pricing: What You Actually Pay in 2026

### LegalZoom pricing

[LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) advertises a $0 Basic package plus state fees, but the Basic tier is thin. As of my latest check, the paid tiers sit at roughly $249 (Standard) and $299 (Premium), and the registered agent service is billed separately at around $249 per year after any included first-year period. Extras such as an EIN, operating agreement and compliance reminders are frequently pushed as paid add-ons during checkout.

My honest opinion: LegalZoom's checkout is where most people overspend. I have watched business owners start with the "free" package and finish with a $400+ cart because each screen presents a plausible-sounding upgrade. Go in knowing which items you actually need.

### BetterLegal pricing

BetterLegal positions itself as a more modern, streamlined alternative. Its formation packages and add-on pricing have changed over time, so I will not quote a number I cannot stand behind. The practical advice: before committing, get the all-in price for **your state** including the registered agent fee for year two, because that renewal cost is where formation services usually differ most. Our [BetterLegal review](/blog/betterlegal-review/) keeps the latest pricing notes.

### The real cost: year two

Formation is a one-time event, but you will owe a registered agent fee every year and, in many states, an annual report or franchise tax. When comparing **BetterLegal vs LegalZoom**, add up:

1. The package price.
2. The state filing fee.
3. The registered agent renewal (LegalZoom's is high relative to competitors; [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) has historically renewed around $119 per year).
4. Any add-ons you need (EIN, operating agreement, expedited filing).

## Speed: Which Files Your LLC Faster?

Speed queries are among the most common I see for this comparison, so it is worth being precise about what "fast" means.

There are two clocks:

- **Provider processing time:** how long the service takes to prepare and submit your documents. Many services do this within one business day, and both LegalZoom and BetterLegal market quick submission.
- **State approval time:** how long the Secretary of State takes. This is outside any provider's control. Some states approve online filings within a day or two; others take several weeks on standard processing.

Because the state clock usually dominates, a provider claiming to be dramatically faster is mostly describing the smaller portion of the timeline. If speed is critical, the lever that actually matters is paying for the **state's expedited option**, which every major provider offers as an add-on, and choosing a state with fast digital processing.

In my experience, the time you lose is rarely on the provider's side. It is in the details you get wrong: a name that conflicts with an existing entity, a mismatched address, or a missing registered agent consent. Double-check those before you submit.

## Features and Services Compared

### Core formation

Both services will prepare and file your Articles of Organization (called a Certificate of Formation in Texas, and a Certificate of Organization in a few other states). Neither replaces legal advice on how to structure your ownership.

### Operating agreement

An operating agreement is not filed with the state in most places, but it is your best evidence of separation between you and the company. LegalZoom's better operating agreement tools sit in the higher-priced packages. Our [LLC operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to include, and our roundup of the [best LLC service with an operating agreement included](/blog/best-llc-service-with-operating-agreement-included/) compares who bundles it.

### Registered agent

Every LLC needs one. You can act as your own, but that puts your address on public record. If privacy matters, see [What Is a Registered Agent?](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) and our guide to the [best registered agent services](/blog/best-registered-agent-services-2026/).

### EIN

You can get an EIN for free directly from the IRS. Any service charging for it is charging for convenience. The [IRS EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) walks through the free application, which takes minutes for most domestic owners. We also cover it in [how to get an EIN free](/blog/how-to-get-an-ein-for-your-llc-free-irs-method/).

### Ongoing compliance

This is where the services diverge most. Ask each provider whether annual report reminders, registered agent mail forwarding and good-standing monitoring are included or extra. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) bundles compliance reminders into its paid tiers, which is one reason I rate it as the stronger long-term option. Read the [ZenBusiness review](/reviews/zenbusiness/) for details.

## Customer Support and User Experience

LegalZoom offers phone and chat support plus optional access to attorney consultations through its broader legal platform, which is a real differentiator if you may need contract or trademark help later. The trade-off is a heavier, upsell-oriented interface.

BetterLegal's appeal is typically a cleaner experience with fewer screens between you and a filed LLC. If you already know your state, structure and name, a leaner flow is genuinely valuable. If you are unsure, more hand-holding is worth paying for.

A practical test: before buying, use each service's chat with a real question, such as "What happens if my chosen name is rejected?" The quality and speed of that answer tells you more than any marketing page.

## BOI Reporting and Other 2026 Compliance Issues

Compliance rules for LLCs have been shifting. FinCEN's Corporate Transparency Act beneficial ownership reporting requirements were significantly narrowed in 2025 for domestic companies, and the details continue to evolve. Check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status rather than relying on any formation service's summary. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) tracks the changes.

What matters for this comparison: some services offer BOI filing help as a paid add-on. Since filing directly with FinCEN is free (see our [FinCEN BOI cost breakdown](/blog/fincen-boi-report-cost-is-it-free/)), only pay if the convenience is worth it to you, and only if the requirement currently applies to your entity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is BetterLegal better than LegalZoom?

It depends on your priorities. BetterLegal is generally positioned as the simpler, faster-feeling option, while LegalZoom offers a broader range of services and support. Compare the all-in price for your state, including the year-two registered agent fee, before deciding.

### Which is cheaper, LegalZoom or BetterLegal?

Both advertise low entry pricing plus state fees. LegalZoom's total often rises through add-ons and a relatively high registered agent renewal. Get an itemized total from each provider for your state instead of comparing headline prices.

### Which service files an LLC faster?

Provider processing is usually quick for both, but the state's approval time is the main factor. To go faster, choose the state's expedited option and make sure your name and address details are correct the first time.

### Do I need a formation service to start an LLC?

No. You can file directly with your Secretary of State, usually for just the state fee. Formation services save time and handle paperwork, but they are optional. Our [What Is an LLC?](/learn/what-is-an-llc/) guide explains the basics.

### Is LegalZoom worth it for an LLC?

It can be, if you value the brand, support and access to other legal services. If cost matters most, compare it with alternatives first. See our full [LegalZoom review](/blog/legalzoom-review-2026/).

### Do I need a registered agent if I use LegalZoom or BetterLegal?

Yes, every LLC needs a registered agent in its state of formation. You can use the service's agent, hire a separate one or serve as your own if you have a physical address in the state and are available during business hours.

### Can I get an EIN for free?

Yes. The IRS issues EINs at no cost. Formation services that charge for it are charging for convenience.
