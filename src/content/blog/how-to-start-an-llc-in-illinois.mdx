## Why Illinois Is a Solid State for Your LLC in 2026

Illinois doesn't get the same buzz as Delaware or Wyoming in LLC formation circles, but for most business owners who actually operate in Illinois, forming here makes far more sense than forming out of state.

**No publication requirement.** New York requires LLCs to publish formation notices in two local newspapers — a process that routinely costs $1,000–$2,000. Illinois has no such requirement. File your Articles of Organization, receive your Certificate of Organization, and you're done.

**No LLC franchise tax.** California hammers LLCs with an $800 minimum franchise tax every year, regardless of revenue. Illinois imposes no comparable fee on LLCs. Your primary recurring state cost is the $75 annual report fee.

**Pass-through taxation by default.** Illinois LLCs are treated as pass-through entities for federal tax purposes. Single-member LLCs file as disregarded entities; multi-member LLCs default to partnership taxation. Profits flow through to members' personal returns and are taxed once, not twice.

**One Illinois-specific detail most guides miss:** the state imposes a 1.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax (PPRT) on net income for LLCs taxed as partnerships. It's not enormous for most small businesses, but it's real — build it into your projections and make sure your accountant knows about it.

In my experience working with small business owners across Illinois, the most common mistake I see isn't a filing error — it's forming in Wyoming or Delaware because someone claimed it would reduce taxes, only to discover they still have to register as a foreign LLC in Illinois where they actually do business. That process costs more, not less, than simply forming in Illinois from day one.

## Step-by-Step: How to Start an LLC in Illinois

### Step 1: Choose and Confirm Your Business Name

Your Illinois LLC name must:
- Include "Limited Liability Company," "LLC," or "L.L.C."
- Be distinguishable from all business names already registered with the Illinois Secretary of State
- Avoid terms implying government affiliation ("Federal," "Treasury," etc.)
- Avoid restricted words like "Bank," "Insurance," or "University" without additional regulatory approvals

Search name availability for free on the [Illinois Secretary of State's ILSOS Business Services portal](https://www.ilsos.gov/). If your preferred name is available but you're not ready to file, you can reserve it for 90 days by paying a $25 name reservation fee.

### Step 2: Appoint a Registered Agent

Every Illinois LLC must designate a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) — a person or business entity with a physical Illinois street address who is available during normal business hours to receive legal documents, state notices, and official correspondence on your LLC's behalf.

You have three options:

1. **Serve as your own registered agent** — free, but your name and address become part of the public record and you must be available at that address during all business hours
2. **Designate someone you trust** — a family member, friend, or business partner with an Illinois address
3. **Hire a professional registered agent service** — typically $50–$125/year; keeps your personal address private and ensures documents are never missed

For most owners, a professional service is the practical choice. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) includes Illinois registered agent service free for the first year on every plan. After year one, it renews at $99/year — a reasonable price for the reliability and privacy it provides.

### Step 3: File Your Articles of Organization

The Articles of Organization is the legal document that creates your LLC. You file it with the Illinois Secretary of State, Business Services Division. This is the core step in how to start an LLC in Illinois, and the state makes it relatively simple.

**What to include:**
- LLC name
- Principal place of business address in Illinois
- Registered agent's name and Illinois street address
- LLC purpose (general purpose language is accepted for most businesses)
- Management structure (member-managed or manager-managed)
- Names and addresses of organizers

**Illinois LLC filing options, fees, and processing times:**

| Method | State Fee | Processing Time |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Online (ILSOS portal) | $150 | 1–2 business days |
| Mail | $150 | 4–6 weeks |
| In-person (Springfield) | $150 | Same day |
| Expedited 24-hour (online) | $150 + $100 | Next business day |

Online filing is the obvious choice for most people. You'll receive your Certificate of Organization by email within a couple of business days.

### Step 4: Create an Operating Agreement

Illinois does not legally require an operating agreement, but you should have one regardless. This internal document defines how your LLC operates — ownership percentages, voting rights, profit and loss distributions, what happens when a member exits, and how the LLC dissolves if needed.

Without one, Illinois's default LLC statutes fill in the blanks, and those defaults may not match what you and your co-owners actually intend. Banks frequently require an operating agreement before opening a business account. Our [LLC Operating Agreement Guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) covers what every agreement should include.

### Step 5: Get an EIN from the IRS

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is your LLC's federal tax ID. You need one if your LLC has more than one member, has employees, or wants to open a business bank account.

Single-member LLCs with no employees can technically use the owner's Social Security number, but getting a separate EIN is good practice — it keeps your SSN off vendor W-9 forms and sharpens the separation between personal and business finances.

Apply free at [IRS.gov](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online). The online process takes about 10 minutes and returns your EIN immediately.

### Step 6: Register for Illinois State Taxes

After forming your LLC, register with the Illinois Department of Revenue (IDOR) through the MyTax Illinois portal (mytax.illinois.gov) if you:
- Have employees (employer withholding tax)
- Sell taxable goods or services in Illinois (sales tax permit)
- Are subject to the Personal Property Replacement Tax

This step is entirely separate from your Secretary of State filing.

### Step 7: Open a Dedicated Business Bank Account

A separate LLC bank account is essential for maintaining your liability protection. Commingling personal and business funds is one of the most reliable ways to lose the liability shield your LLC was formed to provide — courts call it "piercing the corporate veil," and it happens more often than people expect.

To open an account, you'll typically need your Certificate of Organization, EIN, and operating agreement.

### Step 8: File Your BOI Report

Under the Corporate Transparency Act, most LLCs must file a Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) report with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN). Filing requirements and deadlines were updated in 2026 — see our [BOI Report Guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) for current deadlines, exemptions, and how to file.

## Illinois LLC Ongoing Compliance Requirements

Forming an LLC is a one-time process. Staying compliant is ongoing. Here's what Illinois requires in 2026 and beyond.

### Illinois LLC Annual Report

Every Illinois LLC must file an [annual report with the Secretary of State](/blog/illinois-llc-annual-report-filing/) each year. The fee is **$75**. The deadline is before the first day of the month in which your LLC was originally formed — your anniversary month.

**Example:** If you formed your LLC on April 10, 2026, your annual report is due before April 1, 2027 — and before April 1 each year after that.

Missing the deadline triggers late penalties. Extended non-compliance can result in administrative dissolution of your LLC, which requires a reinstatement process to undo.

### Illinois State Tax Obligations

Illinois LLC members are subject to:
- **Illinois individual income tax** at a flat 4.95% rate on their share of LLC income
- **Personal Property Replacement Tax (PPRT)** at 1.5% on the LLC's net income, paid at the entity level for partnership-taxed LLCs
- **Federal self-employment tax** at 15.3% on active business income (you deduct half when calculating adjusted gross income)

If your LLC is generating significant net income — generally in the $60,000–$80,000+ range — explore an S-Corp election with your CPA. Our [LLC vs. S-Corp guide](/learn/llc-vs-s-corp/) breaks down when the election makes financial sense.

### Local Business Licenses

Illinois does not issue a statewide general business license, but local requirements vary widely. Chicago requires a City Business License for most commercial operations, with fees that vary by industry. Many suburban municipalities have their own licensing requirements. Check both your city and county for requirements that apply to your specific business type and location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does it cost to start an LLC in Illinois?**

The minimum cost to start an LLC in Illinois is **$150** — the state filing fee for the Articles of Organization. Add $0–$199 if you use a formation service. Ongoing costs include a $75 annual report fee each year. There is no franchise tax on Illinois LLCs.

**How long does it take to form an LLC in Illinois?**

Online filings through the Illinois Secretary of State's ILSOS portal typically process in **1–2 business days** in 2026. Mail filings take 4–6 weeks. Same-day processing is available by visiting the Springfield office in person, or you can add $100 for expedited 24-hour online processing.

**Does Illinois require a registered agent for an LLC?**

Yes. Illinois law requires every LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical Illinois street address who is available during normal business hours to receive legal documents and state notices. You can serve as your own agent, but most business owners use a professional service to preserve privacy and ensure reliable document handling.

**Does Illinois require an operating agreement for an LLC?**

No — Illinois law does not mandate an operating agreement. However, it's strongly recommended. Without one, Illinois's default LLC statutes govern your internal operations, which may not match your intentions. Banks also routinely require one when you open a business account.

**Does Illinois have a publication requirement for LLCs?**

No. Unlike New York, Illinois does not require LLCs to publish formation notices in any newspaper. Once the Secretary of State accepts your Articles of Organization, the formation process is complete.

**What is the Illinois LLC annual report deadline and fee?**

The annual report fee is **$75** per year. Reports are due before the first day of your LLC's anniversary month — the month in which your LLC was originally formed. Miss that deadline and late penalties apply; extended non-compliance can result in administrative dissolution.

**Can I be my own registered agent in Illinois?**

Yes. Any individual who is at least 18 years old with a physical Illinois street address (P.O. boxes are not accepted) can serve as a registered agent for an Illinois LLC. The tradeoff: your name and address become part of the public record, and you must be available at that address during all normal business hours.

**Do Illinois LLCs pay a franchise tax?**

No. Illinois does not impose a franchise tax on LLCs. The primary recurring state-level costs are the $75 annual report fee and, for LLCs taxed as partnerships, the 1.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax on net income.
