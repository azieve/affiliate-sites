## What Is LegalZoom? Background and Track Record

Founded in 2001 in Los Angeles, LegalZoom has operated for over two decades in the online legal services space. The company went public on Nasdaq in 2021 (ticker: LZM), which gave it the capital to expand its attorney network and compliance infrastructure significantly. As of 2026, LegalZoom has helped form over 4 million businesses and remains one of the few formation services that pairs filing with genuine legal resources.

That broader scope is both LegalZoom's strength and its complexity. Unlike purely transactional services that file your Articles of Organization and send you an invoice, LegalZoom operates more like a legal services platform — with subscriptions, attorney consultations, trademark filings, and ongoing compliance support. Understanding which parts of that ecosystem you actually need is the key to using LegalZoom cost-effectively.

According to [U.S. Census Bureau business formation statistics](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/index.html), new business applications have remained at historically elevated levels since 2020, with hundreds of thousands of LLC filings processed monthly. In that competitive market, LegalZoom's brand recognition still carries weight — but its pricing reflects that premium.

## LegalZoom Pricing and Plans in 2026

LegalZoom's LLC formation pricing in 2026 is structured across three tiers. Your state's filing fee is always separate and on top.

### Basic Plan — $0 + State Filing Fee

The Basic plan covers the core filing: LegalZoom prepares and submits your Articles of Organization. That's the full scope of what's included. There's no operating agreement, no EIN assistance, and no registered agent service bundled in. Registered agent service — which most states legally require — costs an additional **$249/year** if added separately.

For context, [ZenBusiness's Starter plan](/reviews/zenbusiness/) is also $0 + state fees, but it includes a complimentary registered agent for the first year. That single difference represents a $199 savings on year-one costs.

### Pro Plan — $249 + State Filing Fee

The Pro plan is what most users actually need. At $249 (a one-time fee, not a subscription), it adds:

- **Operating agreement** — a template-based document covering ownership structure, management, and voting rights
- **EIN acquisition** — LegalZoom applies for your Employer Identification Number on your behalf
- **Bank account resolution letter** — used to open a business bank account
- **Compliance calendar** — tracks annual report deadlines and renewal dates

This tier is meaningfully more useful than Basic, but the pricing requires scrutiny (more on that below).

### Premium Plan — $299 + State Filing Fee

The Premium plan adds rush processing — LegalZoom prioritizes your filing within their internal workflow. However, it's worth understanding that state processing times are outside any service's control. If your state has a three-week backlog, paying $50 more for "rush" gets your paperwork to the front of LegalZoom's queue, not the state's. For states with fast processing (Wyoming, Delaware, New Mexico), the premium may not be meaningful.

### Ongoing Annual Costs

This is where LegalZoom LLC formation costs compound:

- **Registered Agent Service:** $249/year
- **Business Advantage Legal Plan:** $34.99/month or approximately $179/year (attorney consultations, contract reviews, document library)
- **Annual Report Filing Assistance:** $99–$150/year depending on state (LegalZoom files on your behalf)

A fully-loaded LegalZoom relationship — Pro plan + registered agent + legal subscription — runs approximately **$677 in year one** plus your state filing fee. Compare that to ZenBusiness Pro ($199 + state fees, registered agent included year one), and the gap is substantial.

## LegalZoom Features: The Detailed Breakdown

### Operating Agreement

LegalZoom's operating agreement is a solid, professionally formatted template that covers the fundamentals: member ownership percentages, management structure, capital contributions, and voting procedures. For a single-member LLC or a simple two-partner business, it's legally sufficient in virtually every state.

Where it falls short is customization. The template doesn't accommodate complex ownership arrangements, tiered equity structures, or non-standard distributions without either upgrading to an attorney review (via the legal plan) or modifying the document yourself. For most first-time LLC owners, the standard template is fine. For anything unusual, budget for the legal plan or consult an independent attorney.

### EIN Assistance

LegalZoom handles the EIN application as part of their Pro and Premium plans. The process is straightforward — they collect your information and submit Form SS-4 to the IRS on your behalf.

Here's what's worth knowing: the IRS provides a [free online EIN application](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) that takes about 15 minutes and issues your EIN immediately upon completion. When LegalZoom bundles EIN assistance into their plan pricing, you're paying for convenience — not access. If budget is tight, this is one service you can confidently skip.

### Registered Agent Service

LegalZoom's registered agent service costs $249/year and covers the core requirements: receiving legal documents and official state correspondence, forwarding them to you, and maintaining your registered address. The interface for viewing received documents is clean and functional.

That said, $249/year sits at the high end of the market. [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) offers standalone registered agent service at $125/year with a strong reputation for privacy and professionalism. [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) includes it free in the Pro plan for the first year before charging $199/year. If registered agent cost is a concern, there are better options.

For a primer on what this service actually does and whether you can serve as your own, see our [what is a registered agent guide](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/).

### Attorney Access and Legal Plan

This is LegalZoom's genuine differentiator and the main reason to choose it over alternatives. The Business Advantage plan gives you:

- 30-minute attorney consultations (unlimited, as needed)
- Legal document library (hundreds of contracts, templates, agreements)
- Contract and document review by a licensed attorney
- Business legal Q&A

In my experience reviewing formation services, most entrepreneurs don't need an attorney for the LLC setup itself — but the first year of running a business almost always surfaces at least one situation where a 30-minute call with a lawyer would save you thousands: a vendor contract with unfavorable terms, a client dispute, a lease negotiation, or a partnership disagreement. Having on-demand attorney access for $179/year is genuinely useful for founders in that position.

No other major formation service comes close to this level of integrated legal support. This is the one feature category where LegalZoom is clearly the market leader.

## Hidden Costs and Upsells to Watch For

This is where a fair LegalZoom review 2026 has to get specific. LegalZoom's checkout process is heavily optimized to add services to your order, and it's easy to end up paying significantly more than the advertised plan price without realizing it.

**Upsells commonly pre-checked or prominently surfaced during checkout:**

- Registered agent service ($249/year) — often pre-selected
- Business Advantage legal plan ($34.99/month)
- Business license research report (~$99 one-time)
- Trademark search and application ($199–$599+)
- Business credit builder ($129/year)
- Annual report filing service (varies)

A user who starts on the Basic plan ($0) and accepts the defaults can easily confirm a checkout totaling $500–$700 before state fees. At that price point, the value calculus shifts meaningfully. Competitors like [Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/) and [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) offer more transparent, lower-pressure checkout flows.

**Our recommendation:** Go through the checkout process deliberately. Uncheck every add-on and decide independently whether each service is worth it. In most cases, only the registered agent is genuinely required at formation time.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use LegalZoom

### LegalZoom Is Worth It If You:

- **Want integrated attorney access.** No other formation service offers the depth of legal support LegalZoom provides. If you anticipate needing contract review, legal Q&A, or attorney consultations in your first year, the Business Advantage plan pays for itself quickly.
- **Are forming a more complex entity.** PLLCs, multi-member LLCs with custom operating agreements, or businesses in regulated industries benefit from having legal resources available during formation.
- **Need trademark registration.** LegalZoom's trademark filing service is among the stronger options in this category.
- **Value brand familiarity.** LegalZoom's 25-year track record and 4+ million formations give some founders confidence that a newer service can't match.

### LegalZoom Is Not the Best Choice If You:

- **Are forming a simple single-member LLC.** You're paying a premium for legal features you may never use.
- **Want transparent, upsell-free pricing.** The checkout experience requires active attention to avoid charges you didn't intend.
- **Are focused primarily on registered agent value.** Several services offer lower annual pricing for equivalent coverage.

LegalZoom is not a scam, not a bad service, and not a bad choice. For the right user — specifically, someone who values legal access and is willing to pay for it — LegalZoom delivers genuine value that the formation-only services simply cannot match.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does LegalZoom charge for LLC formation in 2026?

LegalZoom's Basic plan starts at $0 + your state's filing fee. The Pro plan is $249 + state fees, and the Premium plan is $299 + state fees. Registered agent service costs an additional $249/year and is not included in the Basic plan. Always review the checkout page carefully — pre-selected add-ons can significantly increase the total before you confirm.

### Is LegalZoom worth it in 2026?

It depends on your needs. If you anticipate needing attorney consultations, contract reviews, or legal Q&A in your first year of business, LegalZoom's Business Advantage legal plan (~$179/year) can pay for itself quickly. ### How long does LegalZoom take to form an LLC?

LegalZoom's standard processing is typically 7–10 business days from submission on their end, not including state processing time. The Premium plan includes rush filing, which accelerates LegalZoom's internal queue to approximately 3–5 business days. State processing varies — Wyoming and Delaware often process within 1–2 days, while California and New York can take 2–4 weeks during peak periods.

### Does LegalZoom include a registered agent?

Registered agent service is not included in LegalZoom's Basic plan — it costs $249/year as a standalone add-on. Pro and Premium plans may include it as part of limited-time promotions, so always verify at checkout. For comparison, ZenBusiness includes registered agent service free for the first year with the Pro plan.

### What's the difference between LegalZoom's Basic, Pro, and Premium plans?

Basic ($0 + state fee) covers state filing only. Pro ($249 + state fee) adds an operating agreement, EIN application, bank resolution letter, and compliance calendar. Premium ($299 + state fee) adds rush processing and priority service. Registered agent is an annual add-on across all plans unless bundled in a current promotion.

### Can I cancel LegalZoom's registered agent and switch providers?

Yes, you can switch registered agents at any time. You'll need to file a registered agent change form with your state (typically $5–$50) and notify your new agent. Our step-by-step [guide to changing your registered agent](/blog/how-to-change-registered-agent/) covers the full process by state.

Yes. LegalZoom is a publicly traded company (Nasdaq: LZM) that has operated since 2001 and has formed over 4 million businesses. The concerns about LegalZoom center on pricing transparency and upsells — not legitimacy. They have a functional attorney network, working customer support, and a real compliance infrastructure.

### LegalZoom vs. ZenBusiness — which is better for most people?

For most first-time LLC founders without complex legal needs, ZenBusiness is the better choice on value, UI, and total first-year cost. LegalZoom is the better choice specifically when you want attorney access integrated with your formation service.
