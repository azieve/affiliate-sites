## Can a Pakistani Citizen Own a US LLC in 2026?

Yes. No US state requires an LLC member to be a US citizen or resident. A Pakistani national living in Pakistan can be the sole member, a multi-member partner, or the manager of a US LLC. A **Pakistani LLC owner** doesn't need to travel to the US to form the company.

There are two practical limits:

- **You need a US registered agent.** Every state requires a physical address in the state of formation to receive legal documents. Your formation service normally provides this.
- **Your personal address can stay in Pakistan.** The LLC's "principal office" can list your Pakistani address, though many owners prefer to use a registered agent or virtual mailing address for the public record.

I've seen too many first-time international founders assume they need a US partner or a relative with a green card. They don't. What they need is a clear picture of the compliance calendar, which is where most problems start.

## How a US LLC Is Taxed When the Owner Lives in Pakistan

This is the section to read carefully, because the answer is different from what US residents experience.

**A single-member LLC is a "disregarded entity" by default.** The IRS ignores the LLC for income tax purposes and treats the income as yours. For a non-US owner, that creates a specific result:

- If the LLC has **no US trade or business and no US-source income**, it generally owes no US federal income tax. This is the situation for many Pakistani freelancers who perform their services from Pakistan for clients abroad. Income from services performed outside the US is typically treated as foreign-source.
- If the LLC is engaged in a US trade or business (for example, you have employees or inventory in the US), it can have "effectively connected income" that is taxable in the US.

**No tax does not mean no filing.** A foreign-owned single-member LLC must file **Form 5472** with a pro forma Form 1120 each year. The penalty for failing to file is **$25,000 per form**, which is one of the harshest penalties small foreign owners face. The IRS explains the requirement on its [Form 5472 page](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-5472), and our guide to [Form 5472 for foreign-owned single-member LLCs](/blog/form-5472-for-foreign-owned-single-member-llc/) walks through the filing.

**Pakistan taxes you as well.** Pakistani tax residents are generally taxed on worldwide income, so LLC profits flow into your personal Pakistani tax picture. The Federal Board of Revenue ([FBR](https://fbr.gov.pk)) publishes the rules, and special regimes exist for registered IT and IT-enabled services exporters. These rates and conditions change with each federal budget. A US-Pakistan income tax treaty exists, but it is old and limited, so don't assume it removes your obligations in either country. Hire a Pakistani chartered accountant who has handled foreign-company structures. Don't rely on a forum post, including this one.

### Step 5: Get your EIN without an SSN

This is where many Pakistani applicants stall. The IRS online EIN application requires a Social Security Number or ITIN for the responsible party, which you won't have. Instead you apply on **Form SS-4** by fax, mail or phone, entering "Foreign" where the form asks for an SSN, ITIN or EIN of the responsible party. The IRS publishes the process on its [EIN page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers). Expect anywhere from a few days to several weeks. Some formation services offer EIN assistance for a fee. Our guide to [getting an EIN without an SSN](/blog/how-to-get-an-ein-without-an-ssn/) covers the three IRS-approved methods.

### Step 6: Open a bank account and payment processing

You'll need your Articles, EIN letter, operating agreement and passport. Many US digital banks and payment platforms accept foreign-owned LLCs, but their eligibility rules and country lists change, so confirm that they currently accept applicants from Pakistan before you pay for anything. ## Pakistan-Side Compliance: What to Check Before You Start

The US rules are only half the picture. A **US LLC for Pakistani residents** also has to work within Pakistan's foreign-exchange and tax framework. Points to raise with a local professional:

1. **Foreign-exchange rules.** The State Bank of Pakistan ([SBP](https://www.sbp.org.pk)) sets the rules on holding and moving foreign currency. Whether and how a resident individual can hold a foreign business bank account, and how export proceeds must be repatriated, depends on current SBP circulars.
2. **Declaring the asset.** Your LLC interest is generally an overseas asset that should be reflected in your Pakistani tax returns and wealth statement.
3. **Exporter registration.** Freelancers and IT exporters may be able to access concessionary tax treatment by registering and routing proceeds through banking channels. Conditions and rates change, so verify them.
4. **Remitting money home.** Moving LLC profits into Pakistan should follow documented banking channels. Sloppy transfers create problems later, often at the worst possible moment, such as a loan application or tax audit.

I don't want to overstate my certainty here. I'm a US-focused researcher, and Pakistani rules are best confirmed with a local chartered accountant and, for foreign-exchange questions, your authorized dealer bank.

## Do You Need to File a BOI Report in 2026?

Probably not. In March 2025, FinCEN revised its Beneficial Ownership Information rule so that **US-formed companies are no longer required** to file BOI reports, and only certain foreign entities registered to do business in the US are covered. Rules have shifted repeatedly, so check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status before relying on this. Our [BOI report changes for 2026](/blog/boi-report-changes-and-updates-2026/) article tracks the updates. This is a separate matter from Form 5472, which is an IRS requirement that still applies.

## Is a US LLC Right for You? An Honest View

An LLC makes sense if you:

- Invoice US or international clients who prefer a US entity
- Need access to US payment processors, marketplaces or a US business bank account
- Want a liability shield and a credible business identity

It may be unnecessary if you earn modest income from a few clients who pay through Payoneer or direct transfer, since you'll add US filings and costs without much benefit. In my experience, the freelancers who benefit most are those already earning enough that a $500 to $900 annual compliance budget is a small slice of revenue. ## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Skipping Form 5472.** Calendar it from day one. The due date is generally April 15 for calendar-year filers, with an extension available.
- **Using personal and LLC funds interchangeably.** It weakens your liability protection and creates a messy paper trail for both tax authorities.
- **Forming in a state you don't understand.** Delaware's $300 annual tax surprises people who chose it by default.
- **Ignoring your registered agent.** If they can't reach you, a lawsuit notice can slip through. Our [registered agent guide](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) explains why it matters.
- **Assuming "no US tax" means "no tax."** Pakistan still expects its share.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Pakistani resident open a US LLC without visiting the US?

Yes. The entire process, from filing to EIN to banking, can be handled remotely. The EIN is the slowest step because it uses fax, mail or phone instead of the online form.

### How much does it cost to form a US LLC from Pakistan?

State filing fees run from $50 in New Mexico to $100 in Wyoming and $110 in Delaware, and formation services start at $0 on basic tiers. Budget roughly $350 to $900 for year one, including the registered agent, annual filings and Form 5472 preparation.

### Do I need an SSN or ITIN to form an LLC?

No. You don't need either to form the LLC or to obtain an EIN. An ITIN becomes relevant only if you have a US tax return filing requirement. Our guide to [getting an ITIN as a foreign LLC owner](/blog/how-to-get-an-itin-as-a-foreign-llc-owner/) explains when.

### Do I pay US taxes on a US LLC if I live in Pakistan?

It depends on whether the LLC has US-source or effectively connected income. Many Pakistan-based freelancers serving clients from abroad owe no US federal income tax but still must file Form 5472. A tax professional should confirm your situation.

### Which state is best for a Pakistani LLC owner?

Wyoming and New Mexico are popular for low ongoing costs, and Delaware is common for founders planning to raise outside investment. See our [best state to form an LLC](/blog/best-state-to-form-an-llc/) comparison.

### Can I get a US bank account as a Pakistani LLC owner?

Often yes, using fintech banks and payment platforms that support non-resident owners, though acceptance depends on the provider's current policies. Have your Articles, EIN letter, operating agreement and passport ready.

### What happens if I miss the Form 5472 deadline?

The IRS can assess a $25,000 penalty per late or missing form, with additional penalties for continued failure after notice. Filing promptly and requesting abatement for reasonable cause can help, but prevention is far cheaper.

### Is a US LLC the same as a Pakistani private limited company?

No. They are different legal and tax structures under different jurisdictions. A US LLC is governed by state law, while a Pakistani private limited company is registered with the SECP. Each has its own filing and tax regime.
