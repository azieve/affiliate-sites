## ZenBusiness Plans and Pricing for 2026

ZenBusiness offers three core plans. Pricing is competitive, though the real cost depends on which add-ons you select during checkout.

### Starter — $0 + State Fee

The Starter plan covers the bare minimum: ZenBusiness prepares and files your Articles of Organization with the state. You provide the information, they handle the paperwork.

What it does **not** include at this tier:
- Registered agent service (first year discounted, then billed annually)
- Operating agreement
- EIN (Employer Identification Number) obtainment
- Worry-Free Compliance guarantee

For most new business owners, the Starter plan will require several paid add-ons before it actually meets their needs. The effective cost often ends up close to the Pro plan anyway, which is worth factoring in from the start.

### Pro — $199/year

This is ZenBusiness's most popular plan, and for most first-time LLC founders, it's the right starting point. It includes:

- LLC formation filing
- Registered agent service (first year included)
- Operating agreement template
- EIN obtainment
- Worry-Free Compliance (annual report reminders and one filing per year)

The $199/year price point is competitive. For context, [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) charges $39 for formation plus $125/year for registered agent service — so the total first-year cost is comparable, though the structures differ significantly. We break down the head-to-head in our [Northwest vs ZenBusiness comparison](/compare/northwest-vs-zenbusiness/).

### Premium — $299/year

The Premium tier adds rush filing (processing prioritized), a business website and domain, business email, and business document templates beyond the operating agreement. For most LLC owners, the website and domain add-ons are redundant — you can get better hosting elsewhere for less. The rush filing option is the only genuinely useful upgrade at this tier for most users.

**State fees are always additional** regardless of plan. These range from $50 in states like Kentucky and Arkansas to $500+ in Massachusetts and California (which also charges an annual $800 franchise tax minimum). See our full breakdown of [LLC formation costs by state](/learn/how-much-does-an-llc-cost/) before finalizing your budget.

## ZenBusiness Features: What You Actually Get

### Registered Agent Service

Every LLC is legally required to maintain a [registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) — a person or company designated to receive official legal and tax documents on behalf of your business. ZenBusiness's registered agent service is solid. They maintain offices in all 50 states, provide a secure online portal for document delivery, and send timely alerts when official correspondence arrives.

One important note: after the first year, registered agent service renews at $99/year. This isn't hidden, but it's easy to overlook when comparing initial pricing.

### Worry-Free Compliance

This is ZenBusiness's signature feature and one of the more useful offerings in the formation service category. The Worry-Free Compliance guarantee covers:

- Annual report reminders
- One annual report filing per year (included in the Pro plan and above)
- Alerts for state-specific compliance deadlines

For non-lawyers and first-time business owners, missing an annual report filing is a surprisingly common way to fall into "administrative dissolution" — meaning your state effectively treats your LLC as inactive. The IRS's guidance on [business compliance obligations](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed) makes clear that maintaining good standing isn't optional, and ZenBusiness's compliance tooling directly addresses this risk.

### Operating Agreement

ZenBusiness provides a basic operating agreement template on the Pro and Premium plans. It's functional but generic. If your LLC has multiple members, outside investors, or non-standard profit-sharing arrangements, you'll want a lawyer to draft or review your operating agreement rather than relying on a template. That said, for single-member LLCs with straightforward structures, the template is sufficient.

### EIN Obtainment

An EIN (Federal Employer Identification Number) is essentially a Social Security Number for your business — required for opening a business bank account, hiring employees, and filing taxes. ZenBusiness handles EIN obtainment as a service on the Pro plan. The IRS offers this for free directly through their website, but the ZenBusiness option saves you time navigating the IRS portal, which has historically had reliability issues.

### ZenBusiness Money (Business Banking)

ZenBusiness offers a banking product called ZenBusiness Money in partnership with a banking provider. It includes a business checking account, invoicing tools, and expense tracking. I've seen too many new business owners skip the step of separating business and personal finances entirely — which defeats much of the liability protection an LLC provides — so having an integrated banking option is a genuine convenience.

That said, ZenBusiness Money is not a replacement for a full-service business bank account if you have significant transaction volume, need ACH capabilities, or want in-person banking support.

## ZenBusiness Pros and Cons

### Pros

**1. Genuinely competitive pricing at the Pro tier.**
For $199/year all-in (excluding state fees), you get formation, registered agent service, operating agreement, EIN, and compliance monitoring. That's a complete package at a reasonable price.

**2. Clean, user-friendly interface.**
The ZenBusiness dashboard is well-designed. Document uploads, deadline tracking, and correspondence review are straightforward. This matters more than it might seem — compliance slips happen when people can't find or understand their documents.

**3. Strong customer support reputation.**
ZenBusiness offers phone, chat, and email support. Response times are generally good during business hours, and Trustpilot reviews consistently cite support quality as a positive.

**4. Worry-Free Compliance is a differentiator.**
No other formation service in this price range offers as comprehensive a compliance monitoring package. For business owners without legal or accounting support, this feature provides meaningful protection against accidental non-compliance.

**5. All 50 states covered.**
This sounds obvious, but not every service handles all states with equal quality. ZenBusiness operates uniformly across all 50 states and Washington D.C.

### Cons

**1. Aggressive upsells during checkout.**
This is ZenBusiness's most consistent criticism and, honestly, a fair one. The checkout flow presents multiple add-ons — many pre-checked — that can inflate your order significantly if you're not paying close attention. Items like "business contract templates," "Domain Privacy," and "Business License Research" are presented prominently and can easily add $100+ to your initial order.

**2. Registered agent renewal pricing.**
After year one, registered agent service is $99/year. This is reasonable, but the transition from "included" to "billed separately" trips up some users who don't anticipate the renewal charge.

**3. Limited privacy compared to some competitors.**
ZenBusiness's business address service and privacy options are less robust than those offered by [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/), which is specifically built around minimizing the public exposure of founders' personal addresses. For founders who prioritize privacy, this is worth considering.

**4. Template-grade legal documents.**
The operating agreement and other templates are starting points, not finished legal documents. Business owners with complex structures need professional review — ZenBusiness's documents are fine for simple structures, but the service doesn't emphasize this distinction clearly enough.

## Who Should Use ZenBusiness?

ZenBusiness is a strong fit for:

- **First-time LLC founders** who want a guided, clean formation experience without the complexity of navigating state websites directly
- **Solo entrepreneurs and freelancers** forming a single-member LLC for liability protection
- **Business owners who want compliance monitoring** without hiring a lawyer or accountant to track filing deadlines
- **Budget-conscious founders** who need registered agent service included in a formation package

- **Privacy-focused founders** who want maximum insulation of their personal information from public records
- **Multi-member LLCs with complex operating structures** that require custom legal documents
- **Experienced founders** on their second or third entity who understand the process and want bare-bones, low-cost filing without ongoing services

## The BOI Report: What ZenBusiness Doesn't Tell You Clearly Enough

One compliance obligation that deserves explicit attention in any 2026 LLC review: the Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) report required under the Corporate Transparency Act (CTA).

The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) — a bureau of the U.S. Treasury Department — requires most newly formed LLCs to file a BOI report disclosing the beneficial owners of the business. While the enforcement timeline has faced some legal uncertainty (federal courts issued injunctions affecting the original January 2025 deadline), the obligation remains on the regulatory radar and is likely to be enforced going forward.

ZenBusiness does offer BOI filing as a paid add-on. It's worth reviewing whether this is included in your selected plan or priced separately. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) explains the requirement and how to file in detail.
