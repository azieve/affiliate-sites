## Features Breakdown: What Each Plan Actually Delivers

### Formation Speed

Both services submit your Articles of Organization within 1–2 business days of receiving your order. State processing times then vary significantly: states like Wyoming and New Mexico often process same-day or next-day; New York and California can take 3–4 weeks. Both ZenBusiness Premium ($349) and Bizee Platinum ($299) include expedited processing options that can shorten turnaround to 1–7 business days.

### Operating Agreement

Both mid-tier plans — ZenBusiness Pro and Bizee Gold — include a standard operating agreement template. These documents are functional, not attorney-customized, but they satisfy the basic requirement most states recommend for single-member and multi-member LLCs. For background on what should go into one, see our [LLC operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/). If you need a document tailored to a more complex ownership structure, you'd work with an attorney separately.

### EIN Registration

Both services include EIN registration at their $199 mid-tier plans. It's worth noting that you can obtain an EIN directly from the [IRS for free](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) in about 10 minutes — the value these services provide here is pure convenience, not access.

### Compliance Monitoring and Annual Report Support

This is the area where ZenBusiness most clearly pulls ahead in the ZenBusiness vs Bizee (formerly Incfile) matchup. The **Worry-Free Compliance** feature, included in Pro and Premium plans, monitors your LLC's compliance status, sends deadline reminders for annual reports and other state filings, and will file amendments if needed. Bizee's Gold plan includes annual report reminders, but there's no equivalent integrated compliance dashboard.

In my experience advising small business owners, this is the single most underappreciated differentiator. I've seen too many first-time founders let their LLCs fall out of good standing — not because they stopped caring about their business, but because they missed a $50 annual report filing while managing everything else. The consequences can be severe: state-imposed late fees, loss of good standing status, and in some states, administrative dissolution that strips your liability protection. ZenBusiness's compliance tooling directly addresses this failure mode.

### Business Dashboard and Document Storage

ZenBusiness's online dashboard is more polished and feature-complete than Bizee's. It includes document storage, a compliance status tracker, and a guided roadmap for business setup tasks. Bizee's dashboard is functional for tracking your filing status but is less useful as an ongoing business management tool.

## Registered Agent Service: The Hidden Cost That Actually Matters

If you're unclear on why you need a registered agent at all, our guide on [what is a registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) covers it thoroughly. The short version: your LLC is legally required to designate a registered agent with a physical address in your state of formation who receives legal and government correspondence on behalf of your business.

Every LLC formation service — ZenBusiness, Bizee, and their competitors — includes one year of registered agent service as a standard feature. The real cost begins in year two.

- **ZenBusiness:** $199/year after the first year (or included in paid plan subscriptions)
- **Bizee:** $119/year after the first year
- **[Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/):** $125/year as a standalone service

For context, Northwest is widely considered one of the most privacy-focused and reliable registered agent providers in the country. Its $125/year standalone rate is competitive with Bizee's renewal and significantly below ZenBusiness's. If registered agent cost is your primary concern after year one, both Bizee and Northwest offer better rates than ZenBusiness's standalone service.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bizee the same as Incfile?

Yes. Incfile rebranded to Bizee in 2023. The services, pricing structure, and operational team are the same — the company simply changed its name. Any older content you find referencing "Incfile" is describing the same business now operating as Bizee.

### Does ZenBusiness or Bizee have a better free plan?

Both free plans (ZenBusiness Starter and Bizee Silver) are closely comparable: you get Articles of Organization filed and one year of free registered agent service. The more important comparison is year two and beyond: Bizee's registered agent renewal ($119/yr) is substantially cheaper than ZenBusiness's standalone rate ($199/yr).

### Can I switch registered agents after the first year to save money?

Yes. You can change your registered agent at any time by filing a straightforward form with your state, typically at a cost of $5–$50 depending on the state. Many business owners form through ZenBusiness or Bizee for the free first year, then transfer to a standalone service like [Northwest Registered Agent](/go/northwest/) for ongoing years.

### Does ZenBusiness include an operating agreement?

ZenBusiness includes an operating agreement template with its Pro ($199/year) and Premium ($349/year) plans. The Starter plan does not include one. If you need an operating agreement on the free plan, you can draft one from a reputable template source or engage an attorney for a customized version.

### What is the ZenBusiness Worry-Free Compliance guarantee?

The Worry-Free Compliance feature, included in ZenBusiness Pro and Premium plans, monitors your LLC's compliance status, sends reminders for key deadlines like annual reports, and covers any state-imposed fees if ZenBusiness misses a deadline on their end. It's a meaningful differentiator for business owners who don't want to manage a compliance calendar manually — and it's one of the main reasons ZenBusiness commands a higher renewal price than Bizee.

### How long does LLC formation take with ZenBusiness vs Bizee?

Both services submit your Articles of Organization within 1–2 business days of receiving your order. State processing times are the variable: Wyoming and New Mexico often process in 1–3 business days; California and New York can take several weeks. Expedited processing, available in both services' top-tier plans, typically reduces turnaround to 1–7 business days.

### Which service is cheaper over three years?

For pure cost comparison, Bizee tends to come out ahead over three years — primarily due to its lower registered agent renewal rate ($119/yr vs $199/yr). However, this calculation assumes you don't need ZenBusiness's compliance features. State fines for missed annual report deadlines typically range from $50–$200 or more; if ZenBusiness's compliance tools help you avoid even one missed filing, the cost gap narrows considerably.

### Do I need a formation service, or can I file my LLC myself?

You can absolutely file your LLC directly with your state — the process involves submitting Articles of Organization and paying the state filing fee, with no intermediary required. The Small Business Administration's [guide to forming an LLC](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure) walks through the process state by state. Formation services add value through convenience, registered agent bundling, compliance reminders, and document templates — not through access to anything you couldn't reach on your own.
