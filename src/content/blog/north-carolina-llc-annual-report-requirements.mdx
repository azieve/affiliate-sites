## North Carolina LLC Annual Report Requirements at a Glance

Here is the short version of the North Carolina LLC annual report requirements for 2026:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who files | Every domestic LLC and foreign LLC registered in NC |
| Filed with | North Carolina Secretary of State |
| Filing fee | $200 per report |
| Due date | April 15 each year |
| First report due | April 15 of the year after the LLC is formed or registered |
| Filing window | Opens January 1 |
| Filing method | Online (fastest), or by mail |
| Penalty for non-filing | Possible administrative dissolution after notice |

Note that North Carolina does not tie the due date to your formation anniversary. Every LLC files by April 15, whether it formed in February or November.

## Who Has to File an Annual Report in North Carolina?

Every LLC on the Secretary of State's active records must file, including:

- **Single-member LLCs**, even if the company had no revenue or activity.
- **Multi-member LLCs** of any size.
- **Foreign LLCs** (formed elsewhere but registered to do business in North Carolina).
- **Professional LLCs (PLLCs)** such as those held by physicians, attorneys, or accountants.

There is no "dormant company" exemption. An inactive LLC still has to file until it is formally dissolved. I've seen too many owners treat a side-project LLC as "basically closed" because it stopped earning money. It was still on the state's books, still owed the fee, and eventually ended up in dissolution status. If you're done with the business, dissolve it properly using our [guide to dissolving an LLC](/blog/how-to-dissolve-an-llc/) rather than letting it lapse.

## NC LLC Annual Report Deadline: April 15

The **NC LLC annual report deadline** is April 15 each year. The Secretary of State's online system opens for filing on January 1, so you can file any time in that window.

My practical advice: don't wait until April. Put a calendar reminder for the first week of January, file early, and you'll never think about it again. An early filing also means you find any problems, such as a registered agent who has resigned or an outdated address, while you still have time to fix them.

Your first report is due April 15 of the year following formation. An LLC formed in October 2025, for example, owes its first report by April 15, 2026. An LLC formed in October 2026 owes its first report by April 15, 2027.

## What Information Does the Annual Report Require?

The report is brief. Gather the following before you sit down to file:

1. **LLC legal name and SOSID**, the Secretary of State ID number assigned at formation.
2. **Registered agent name and registered office address.** This must be a physical North Carolina street address, not a P.O. box.
3. **Principal office address and mailing address.**
4. **Company officials**, meaning the managers or members and their addresses.
5. **Signature and contact details** of the person completing the filing.

If your registered agent or address has changed, you can update it through the proper change filing. The annual report is also a public record, so officials' names and addresses will appear on it. If privacy matters to you, read our guide to [forming an anonymous LLC](/blog/anonymous-llc-how-to-form-one/) before choosing which addresses to list.

## How to File Your North Carolina LLC Annual Report (Step by Step)

1. **Find your SOSID.** Search your company on the North Carolina Secretary of State's business registration site. Your SOSID is on your Certificate of Formation.
2. **Go to the online annual report filing.** The North Carolina Secretary of State's website ([sosnc.gov](https://www.sosnc.gov/)) hosts the filing portal for LLC annual reports.
3. **Verify your company details.** Review the registered agent, addresses, and officials already on file.
4. **Update anything that changed.** Enter current manager or member information.
5. **Pay the $200 fee.** Use a credit or debit card.
6. **Save your confirmation.** Download the filed report and store it with your LLC records, next to your [operating agreement](/blog/llc-operating-agreement-template-and-guide/).

Filing by mail is possible but slower, and you won't get instant confirmation. I recommend online filing unless you have a specific reason not to.

## What Happens If You Miss the Deadline?

Failing to file does not trigger a large automatic late fee in the way some states do. The risk is more serious: the Secretary of State can begin administrative dissolution proceedings against an LLC that has not filed its annual reports. This is the heart of **NC LLC administrative dissolution** risk.

Consequences of dissolution can include:

- Loss of "good standing," which can block bank loans, new accounts, or contract signings.
- Weakened liability protection if the company keeps operating while dissolved.
- Difficulty registering the name, since a dissolved LLC's name can eventually become available to others.
- Extra cost to reinstate: you will generally have to file the overdue reports, pay the fees, and submit a reinstatement application.

Here is a worked example. Say you skip two years of reports on a company you thought was dormant. Reinstating it would involve $400 in back annual report fees at the $200 rate, plus any reinstatement fees the state charges. That is far more than the cost of filing on time. Confirm the exact reinstatement fee and process with the Secretary of State before you file.

## Annual Report vs. Franchise Tax vs. BOI Report

Owners often confuse three different obligations:

- **The annual report** is a state filing with the Secretary of State, due April 15, with a $200 fee.
- **State income and franchise taxes** are handled by the North Carolina Department of Revenue. A default LLC taxed as a pass-through does not pay the corporate franchise tax, but an LLC that elects corporate tax treatment may. Talk to a CPA about your situation.
- **The federal BOI report** is a FinCEN filing. Following FinCEN's 2025 interim final rule, domestic U.S. companies are generally exempt from BOI reporting, but the rules have shifted repeatedly, so check [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status.

For a clear comparison, see our article on the [BOI report vs. annual report](/blog/boi-report-vs-annual-report-what-is-the-difference/). Federal tax obligations are separate again. The IRS explains how LLCs are classified on its [LLC page](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does North Carolina require an annual report for LLCs?

Yes. Every active domestic and foreign LLC registered in North Carolina must file an annual report with the Secretary of State by April 15 each year. The first report is due April 15 of the year after formation.

### How much is the North Carolina LLC annual report fee?

The state fee is $200 per report. A third-party service may charge extra for preparing or filing it on your behalf.

### When is the NC LLC annual report due?

April 15 every year. The online filing window opens January 1, so you can file well ahead of the deadline.

### What happens if I don't file my North Carolina LLC annual report?

Your LLC can lose good standing and may be administratively dissolved after the state sends notice. You would then need to file overdue reports and apply for reinstatement. Operating while dissolved can jeopardize your liability protection.

### Do I need to file an annual report if my LLC made no money?

Yes. The requirement applies to every LLC on the state's active records, regardless of revenue or activity. If you no longer want the LLC, formally dissolve it instead.

### Can I file the North Carolina LLC annual report online?

Yes. Online filing through the North Carolina Secretary of State's website is the fastest method and gives you immediate confirmation.

### Is the annual report the same as a BOI report?

No. The annual report goes to North Carolina's Secretary of State. The BOI report is a separate federal FinCEN requirement, and its scope for domestic companies has narrowed significantly. See our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) for the latest.

### Can a registered agent file the annual report for me?

Some registered agent and compliance services offer annual report filing, usually for an added fee or within a higher-tier plan. Check what your plan includes before assuming it is covered.
