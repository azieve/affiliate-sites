## LegalZoom vs Harbor Compliance: Quick Verdict

Here is the short version before the detail:

- **Choose [LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/)** if you want a recognizable brand, a guided online ordering process, and optional add-ons such as attorney consultations, trademark filings, and tax help under one roof.
- **Choose Harbor Compliance** if you operate in several states, need foreign qualifications, annual reports, business licenses, or nonprofit filings, and care more about ongoing compliance than a cheap day-one filing.
- **Choose [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/)** if you are a typical single-state startup that wants low upfront cost, fast filing, and compliance reminders without paying specialist rates.

In my experience, the biggest mistake first-time founders make is buying a compliance-heavy service when they only need to file one LLC, or buying the cheapest filing package when they will soon be registered in four states. Match the tool to the stage you are actually at.

## LegalZoom Overview: Pricing, Features, and Gotchas

LegalZoom has been around since 2001 and is publicly traded, which gives it a level of brand recognition most competitors lack. Its LLC formation packages are tiered, typically moving from a basic tier with a $0 service fee (you still pay the state filing fee) up through mid-level and premium tiers that add things like an operating agreement, EIN assistance, and a first year of registered agent service.

**What LegalZoom does well**

- Polished ordering experience that walks you through each step.
- Large menu of adjacent services: trademarks, wills, contracts, and an attorney-advice network.
- Registered agent service available nationwide, with a free first year on higher tiers.

**Gotchas to watch**

- The basic tier is often a "get you started" option. Items many founders consider essential, such as an operating agreement or EIN help, can push you into a pricier tier.
- Registered agent renewal after the free year is a recurring charge that often exceeds what specialist providers charge. Mark your renewal date.
- Upsells during checkout are frequent. Read each screen carefully, because add-ons you do not need are easy to click through.

## Harbor Compliance Overview: Who It Is Really For

Harbor Compliance positions itself less as a "form your LLC fast" shop and more as an ongoing compliance partner. Its service menu centers on:

- **Registered agent service** in all 50 states.
- **Foreign qualification** (registering your existing LLC to do business in another state).
- **Annual report and renewal filings**, along with a compliance calendar to track deadlines.
- **Business and professional licensing** research and filing.
- **Nonprofit formation** and related state and federal filings.

This makes it a natural fit when your structure is more complicated than a single LLC in a single state. If you run an e-commerce company with employees in three states, or a growing services firm expanding region by region, a single compliance partner can reduce the chance of a missed filing.

**Trade-offs**

- It is generally not the cheapest route for a bare-bones, one-state LLC.
- The experience is more consultative than the instant checkout flow at consumer-oriented brands, which some founders like and others find slower.
- Pricing is often service-by-service, so your total depends on how many states and filings you need.

A registered agent receives legal notices and official state mail on behalf of your LLC. Our guide to [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) covers the basics, but the key point for this comparison is that you need one in every state where your LLC is formed or registered.

- **LegalZoom** often bundles the first year free with higher formation tiers, then bills annually at its standard rate.

## Multi-State Compliance: Where Harbor Compliance Pulls Ahead

This is the area where the LegalZoom vs Harbor Compliance decision is clearest. If your LLC "does business" in more than one state, most states require you to register as a foreign LLC, file annual reports there, and keep a local registered agent. Missing these can lead to fines, loss of good standing, and even loss of the ability to sue in that state's courts until you cure the problem.

Harbor Compliance's workflow is built around exactly this. A tracked compliance calendar and per-state filings reduce the administrative load for a business operating across state lines. LegalZoom can help with many of the same filings, but it is more of a generalist, and its strengths lie elsewhere.

For the legal side of expanding, read our guide to [foreign LLC registration](/blog/foreign-llc-registration-doing-business-in-another-state/) and our picks for the [best LLC service for a multi-state business](/blog/best-llc-service-for-multi-state-business/).

**A worked example:** Imagine a consulting LLC formed in Delaware that begins serving clients in Texas and California in 2026. It now has to evaluate foreign registration in both states, appoint registered agents there, and track separate annual obligations. California alone has an $800 minimum annual franchise tax for LLCs, as we explain in our [California franchise tax guide](/blog/california-llc-800-franchise-tax/). A founder who forgets one of these deadlines will pay in penalties. That is the scenario where a compliance specialist earns its fee.

## Hidden Costs and Things to Verify Before You Buy

Whichever provider you choose, check these items:

1. **State filing fees are separate.** They range widely by state, from roughly $35 to $500 for formation. See our [LLC cost guide](/learn/how-much-does-an-llc-cost/).
2. **Registered agent renewal pricing.** A free first year is a marketing hook; the renewal rate is what you will live with.
3. **Annual report filing fees.** Some providers charge a service fee on top of the state fee.
4. **Operating agreement.** Confirm whether it is included or an add-on. Our [operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains why it matters, even for single-member LLCs.
5. **Beneficial ownership reporting.** FinCEN's rules have changed repeatedly. In 2025, FinCEN issued an interim final rule that removed the requirement for U.S.-formed companies and their owners to file Beneficial Ownership Information reports. Check [FinCEN's official BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) for the current status, and see our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) for context.
6. **EIN.** You can get one free directly from the IRS. Do not pay a service to do what the [IRS EIN application](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) does at no cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Harbor Compliance better than LegalZoom?
Neither is universally better. Harbor Compliance is stronger for multi-state compliance, foreign qualifications, licensing, and nonprofits. LegalZoom is stronger for brand familiarity and a broad menu of consumer legal services. For a simple single-state LLC, [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/) is often the better value.

### How much does LegalZoom charge to form an LLC?
LegalZoom's tiered packages have started with a $0 service fee on the basic tier, with mid and premium tiers costing a few hundred dollars. You also pay your state's filing fee. Pricing changes, so check the current plan page.

### Does Harbor Compliance form LLCs?
Yes. Harbor Compliance offers LLC and nonprofit formation, but its main strength is ongoing compliance, registered agent service, and multi-state filings rather than low-cost, bare-bones formation.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?
Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in that state. You can serve as your own agent, but many owners prefer a commercial service for privacy and reliability. Read our [registered agent guide](/blog/what-is-a-registered-agent-and-do-i-need-one/).

### What happens if I miss an annual report deadline?
Consequences vary by state but typically include late fees, loss of good standing, and potentially administrative dissolution. See our guide on [what happens if you don't renew your LLC](/blog/what-happens-if-you-don-t-renew-your-llc/).

### Can I switch registered agents later?
Yes. You can change your registered agent by filing a form with the state. Our [step-by-step guide to changing your registered agent](/blog/how-to-change-registered-agent/) walks through it.

### Which is cheaper, LegalZoom or Harbor Compliance?
For a one-state LLC, LegalZoom's basic tier usually has the lower entry price. For ongoing multi-state compliance, total cost depends on how many states and filings you need, so request a quote and compare the full annual cost, not just the formation fee.

### Is it worth paying for compliance services at all?
It depends on complexity. If you operate in one state and are comfortable calendaring a single annual report, a low-cost provider is usually enough. If you cross state lines, the cost of a missed filing can outweigh a compliance fee.
