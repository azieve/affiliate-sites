LegalZoom put online LLC formation on the map, but it isn't the cheapest option anymore, and for a lot of small business owners in 2026, it isn't even the best one. Between rising renewal fees, upsells during checkout, and registered agent pricing that jumps after year one, more founders are shopping around before they file. If you're looking for solid **LegalZoom alternatives for LLC formation**, you have more (and better) options than you might think — starting with [ZenBusiness](/go/zenbusiness/), which offers a $0-plus-state-fee starter plan and consistently edges out LegalZoom in independent testing for value and customer support.

I've helped dozens of clients pick between formation services over the years, and the pattern is consistent: people default to LegalZoom because it's the name they've heard of, then feel a little burned when the renewal invoice arrives. That's not a knock on LegalZoom specifically — it's a decent service — it's just rarely the *best* deal for a straightforward single-member or multi-member LLC. This guide breaks down seven legitimate LegalZoom alternatives for LLC owners, with real pricing tiers, what's actually included, and where each one falls short, so you can make an informed choice instead of a default one.

## Why LLC Owners Are Looking for LegalZoom Alternatives in 2026

Business formation volume has stayed near record highs since the pandemic-era surge, with the U.S. Census Bureau's [Business Formation Statistics](https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/index.html) showing millions of new business applications filed every year. That volume has fueled competition — dozens of formation companies now fight for the same customer LegalZoom used to have mostly to itself, which is good news for you as a buyer.

A few specific frustrations keep coming up when clients ask me about LegalZoom competitors:

- **Renewal price jumps.** LegalZoom's registered agent service and compliance tools often renew at a higher rate than the promotional first-year price, which isn't always obvious at checkout.
- **Upsell-heavy checkout flow.** Add-ons for EIN filing, operating agreements, and expedited processing can push a "$0" formation package to $300+ before you finish checking out.
- **Slower turnaround on some state filings** compared to competitors who specialize in speed.
- **Limited compliance alerts** compared to services built around ongoing compliance monitoring, which matters more in 2026 given the added scrutiny around beneficial ownership reporting (more on that below).

None of this makes LegalZoom a bad choice — it's a mature, publicly recognizable brand with real customer service infrastructure. But "recognizable" and "best value" aren't the same thing, and that gap is exactly why LegalZoom alternatives for LLC formation have gained so much market share over the past few years.

## What to Look for in a LegalZoom Alternative

Before comparing specific companies, it helps to know which features actually matter. As a lawyer, I tell clients to evaluate any LLC formation service — LegalZoom or otherwise — against these criteria:

1. **Total first-year cost**, including the state filing fee, registered agent service, and any "required" add-ons that aren't optional in practice (like an EIN, which you need to open a business bank account).
2. **Registered agent pricing after year one.** This is where many services quietly raise prices. Our guide to [what a registered agent actually does](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) explains why you can't skip this service in most states.
3. **Operating agreement inclusion.** A free template is not the same as a customized document; some services genuinely tailor it to your management structure.
4. **Compliance monitoring**, especially annual report reminders and — as of 2026 — awareness of ongoing Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting obligations under the Corporate Transparency Act.
5. **Refund policy and customer support responsiveness**, which matters more than people expect when a state filing gets rejected for a technicality.

### 2. LegalZoom — Best for Brand Recognition and Add-On Legal Services

[LegalZoom](/go/legalzoom/) still earns a spot on this list because it's not a bad service — it's just not the value leader anymore. The Basic plan starts at $0 plus state fees, with Pro (around $249) and Premium (around $299) tiers adding registered agent service, operating agreements, and compliance alerts. Where LegalZoom genuinely stands out is its broader legal services ecosystem: trademark filing, attorney consultations, and estate planning documents are all available under one account, which some business owners find genuinely convenient if they want a one-stop shop.

If your priority is name recognition (for example, you're pitching investors and want a formation partner they'll recognize) or you anticipate needing adjacent legal services like trademark registration, LegalZoom remains a reasonable secondary choice. Read our full [LegalZoom review](/reviews/legalzoom/) for the complete pricing breakdown.

**Best for:** Business owners who want legal services beyond formation under one brand.

### 3. Tailor Brands — Best for Branding Bundled With Formation

Tailor Brands built its reputation on logo design and branding tools, and it has since expanded into full LLC formation. Plans run from a Lite tier (around $0 plus state fees) up to Premium (around $249/year), and the standout feature is that formation, a logo, and basic branding assets come from the same platform. Unlike LegalZoom, which treats branding as an entirely separate product line, Tailor Brands designs the experience so your LLC paperwork and your business identity get created in the same workflow.

The tradeoff: Tailor Brands' compliance tools are less robust than ZenBusiness's, and some reviewers note the branding tools feel more like a marketing funnel toward paid design upgrades than a core formation service. See our [Tailor Brands review](/blog/tailor-brands-llc-review/) for a full breakdown.

**Best for:** Solo founders who want a logo and brand kit at the same time they form their LLC.

### 4. Inc Authority — Best Free LLC Formation Option

[Inc Authority](/go/inc-authority/) is one of the few services that genuinely forms your LLC for $0 — you only pay the state filing fee, with no hidden "processing fee" tacked on at checkout. The catch is that the free tier is intentionally basic; a registered agent is included for the first year, but most owners end up paying for at least one paid tier (Starter Plus or Fast Track, typically $99–$225 plus state fees) to get an operating agreement, EIN, and banking resolution documents included.

Compared to LegalZoom's $0 tier, which still nudges you toward paid add-ons throughout checkout, Inc Authority's upsell path is a little more transparent about what's actually free versus paid.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious founders who are comfortable handling some paperwork themselves.

Our guide to [forming an anonymous LLC](/blog/anonymous-llc-how-to-form-one/) covers this strategy in more depth.

Northwest's registered agent service renews at $125/year, which is higher than some competitors, but the company is well known for actually answering the phone — a detail that matters more than people expect when a state filing gets kicked back. Read our full [Northwest Registered Agent review](/reviews/northwest-registered-agent/) for the complete picture, or see how it stacks up in our [Northwest vs. ZenBusiness comparison](/compare/northwest-vs-zenbusiness/).

**Best for:** Home-based business owners who want their address kept off public records.

### 6. Bizee — Best Ongoing Free Registered Agent

[Bizee](/go/bizee/) (formerly Incfile) offers a $0 formation package that includes a full year of free registered agent service — a genuine differentiator, since most competitors, including LegalZoom, charge for registered agent service from day one on paid tiers. After year one, Bizee's registered agent renews at roughly $119/year, which is competitive but not the cheapest on this list.

Bizee's paid tiers (Fast and Premium, roughly $299–$449) add expedited filing and business document templates. It's a solid mid-tier alternative, though its customer support has historically been slower to respond than ZenBusiness's. Full details are in our [Bizee review](/reviews/bizee/).

### A Note on BOI Reporting in 2026

Regardless of which LegalZoom alternative you choose, most LLCs formed in the U.S. are still required to file a Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) report with FinCEN under the Corporate Transparency Act, unless your entity qualifies for an exemption. The rules and enforcement posture around this requirement have shifted multiple times since 2024, so don't rely on outdated information — check the current guidance directly on [FinCEN's BOI page](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) before assuming you're covered or exempt. Some formation services (ZenBusiness among them) now include BOI filing assistance in their compliance packages, which is worth factoring into your decision if you'd rather not track the deadline yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best LegalZoom alternative for LLC formation in 2026?**
ZenBusiness is generally the best overall alternative, thanks to its $0 starter plan, transparent renewal pricing, and built-in compliance monitoring. LegalZoom remains a solid secondary option if you want a recognizable brand or plan to use additional legal services down the road.

**Is ZenBusiness actually cheaper than LegalZoom?**
For most first-year formations, yes — ZenBusiness's paid tiers tend to include more (registered agent, operating agreement) at a comparable or lower price than LegalZoom's equivalent tiers. Always compare the exact plan contents, since both companies adjust pricing periodically.

**Do I need a registered agent if I use a formation service?**
Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in the state of formation. Most formation services, including all seven listed here, include this service for at least the first year — check our guide on [what a registered agent is](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) for the full requirement breakdown.

**Can I switch away from LegalZoom to a different registered agent later?**
Yes, and it's a straightforward process in every state — you file a change-of-agent form (sometimes bundled into your annual report) and pay a small fee. You are not locked into your original formation provider's registered agent service indefinitely.

**Are "free" LLC formation services like Inc Authority and Bizee actually free?**
The formation filing itself can genuinely be $0 plus your state's mandatory filing fee (which ranges from about $35 to $500 depending on the state). However, most owners end up paying for at least one add-on — an EIN filing service, an operating agreement, or expedited processing — so budget for some incremental cost beyond the advertised "free" tier.

**How long does it take to form an LLC with these services?**
Standard processing typically takes 1–3 weeks depending on your state's backlog, though most of these services offer expedited processing (often 1–2 business days) for an added fee. State processing times, not the formation company, are usually the bottleneck.

**Do I still need to file a BOI report if I use one of these services?**
In most cases, yes, unless your LLC qualifies for a specific exemption under the Corporate Transparency Act. This is a federal filing obligation separate from state-level formation, and the requirement and its enforcement have been updated more than once, so confirm current status through [FinCEN.gov](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) rather than relying on older articles.

**Which LegalZoom alternative is best for a single-member LLC with a simple structure?**
ZenBusiness or Bizee tend to be the best fit for straightforward, single-member LLCs, since neither requires you to pay for complexity you don't need. Save the attorney-reviewed option (LLC Attorney) for situations involving multiple members or unusual ownership arrangements.
