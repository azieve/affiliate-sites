## Features That Matter After You Form Your LLC

Forming an LLC takes a few days. Keeping it healthy takes years. A CFO's rule of thumb: the formation fee is a rounding error compared with the cost of administrative dissolution, lost liability protection, or late-filing penalties. These are the features that actually protect you.

### Annual reports and compliance

Most states require an annual or biennial report, and missing it can lead to late fees, loss of good standing, and eventually administrative dissolution. ZenBusiness's paid tiers include deadline monitoring and filing assistance. Tailor Brands offers reminders, but its compliance tooling is lighter. If you operate in a state like Florida or Texas, where annual obligations are strictly enforced, this matters. See our guides to [starting an LLC in Texas](/start-llc/texas/) or [Florida](/start-llc/florida/).

### Operating agreement

An operating agreement is not always legally required by the state, but banks often ask for it and it is your first line of defense in a member dispute. Both services provide a template on higher tiers. Neither is a substitute for a lawyer if you have multiple members or unequal ownership. Our [operating agreement guide](/learn/llc-operating-agreement/) explains what to include.

### Beneficial ownership reporting (BOI)

The rules around FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting changed significantly in 2025, when the Treasury Department announced that domestic U.S. companies would be exempt from reporting under an interim final rule. Check [FinCEN's current guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/boi) before you assume anything, since this area has shifted repeatedly. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) tracks the changes. In practice, neither provider's BOI service should be a deciding factor for a domestic LLC in 2026.

### Banking and next steps

ZenBusiness's Premium tier layers in banking-related perks and business tools, which helps if you plan to open a business account right away. Tailor Brands emphasizes a website and brand kit. ## A Lawyer's Perspective: What Neither Service Replaces

A senior corporate lawyer would stress one thing: formation services are filing agents, not legal advisors. They submit your paperwork. They do not tailor your structure to your tax situation, your liability exposure, or your partners.

Three points worth understanding:

1. **Liability protection depends on behavior.** An LLC shields personal assets from business debts, but courts can "pierce the veil" if you mix personal and business funds or ignore formalities. Open a separate bank account and keep clean records. Our [LLC vs sole proprietorship](/learn/llc-vs-sole-proprietorship/) guide explains the difference.
2. **Tax treatment is your choice, not the provider's.** By default, a single-member LLC is taxed as a sole proprietorship and a multi-member LLC as a partnership. Electing S-corp status may reduce self-employment tax once profits are high enough, but it adds payroll obligations. The [IRS explains LLC classification](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/limited-liability-company-llc) on its website. Speak to a CPA before electing.
3. **Multi-member LLCs need a custom operating agreement.** A template is a starting point, not a finished product. If you are splitting ownership with partners, hire an attorney.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ZenBusiness better than Tailor Brands?

For most new LLC owners, yes. ZenBusiness offers stronger compliance support, including annual report monitoring on its paid tiers, a clearly tiered structure, and a long track record as a formation specialist. Tailor Brands is better if branding tools are your priority.

### How much does it cost to form an LLC with ZenBusiness vs Tailor Brands?

Both have entry options with a $0 service fee plus your state's filing fee. Paid tiers add a registered agent, operating agreement, and other features. ZenBusiness's paid tiers have historically been about $199 and $349 per year. Prices and promotions change, so confirm current figures at checkout.

### Do I need a registered agent for my LLC?

Yes. Every state requires an LLC to maintain a registered agent with a physical address in the state of formation. You can act as your own agent, use a commercial service, or use the agent bundled with your formation package. See our [registered agent guide](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/).

### Does Tailor Brands file the LLC with the state for me?

Yes, Tailor Brands prepares and submits your Articles of Organization to the state. The same is true for ZenBusiness. The difference is in what each bundles after filing.

### What happens if I miss my LLC's annual report?

Consequences vary by state, but typically include late fees and, if the problem continues, administrative dissolution, which can strip your liability protection. This is why compliance reminders matter. See [What Happens If You Don't Renew Your LLC?](/blog/what-happens-if-you-don-t-renew-your-llc/).

### Can I form an LLC myself instead of using a service?

Yes. You can file directly with your Secretary of State and avoid service fees. The trade-off is time and the risk of errors. Many people use a service for convenience and compliance support.

### Do I still need to file a BOI report in 2026?

Rules changed significantly in 2025, and domestic companies were largely exempted under a FinCEN interim final rule. Because the situation has evolved, check FinCEN's website directly or consult a professional. Our [BOI report guide](/learn/boi-report-guide/) tracks updates.

### Which is the best LLC formation service overall?

It depends on your needs, but ZenBusiness is our top overall pick for most founders, with LegalZoom as a solid alternative if you want a bigger brand. See [ZenBusiness vs LegalZoom](/blog/zenbusiness-vs-legalzoom/) for details.
