## What a Registered Agent Actually Does (and Why It's Not Optional)

Every state requires an LLC to designate a registered agent — a person or company with a physical street address in the state of formation who is available during business hours to receive service of process (lawsuits), state compliance notices, and official government mail on the company's behalf. This isn't a marketing add-on; it's a legal requirement. The U.S. Small Business Administration covers the basics of this obligation in its [guide to choosing a business structure](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/choose-business-structure), and every Secretary of State office enforces it.

You *can* serve as your own registered agent, but most founders shouldn't. Your name and home address become part of the public record, you have to be physically present during business hours, and if you miss a service-of-process delivery, you can lose a lawsuit by default. That's the whole reason commercial registered agent services exist — and why both Northwest and Bizee build their recurring revenue around it. For a fuller primer, our [what is a registered agent](/learn/what-is-a-registered-agent/) guide walks through the role step by step.

The key features to compare between any two registered agent services are: annual renewal price, how mail is scanned and forwarded, privacy protection on public filings, compliance and deadline alerts, and the quality of human support when something goes wrong. Northwest and Bizee handle each of these very differently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Northwest or Bizee cheaper for registered agent service?

Bizee is slightly cheaper at $119/year versus Northwest's $125/year, and Bizee includes the first year free with formation. Over a three-year window, Bizee runs about $238 versus Northwest's $375. However, Northwest includes privacy protection at no extra charge, which Bizee treats as an add-on — so if privacy matters to you, the real-world cost gap narrows significantly.

### Can I use a registered agent service that didn't form my LLC?

Yes. The registered agent and the formation company are completely independent decisions. You can form your LLC yourself or through one provider and hire a separate registered agent. You can also switch registered agents at any time by filing a change-of-agent form with your Secretary of State, usually for a small fee or free.

### Does Bizee's free first year of registered agent service auto-renew?

Yes. Bizee's free first year automatically converts to a paid $119/year renewal unless you cancel or switch agents before the renewal date. Set a calendar reminder for roughly 11 months after formation so the charge doesn't surprise you. This auto-renewal is the most common "gotcha" founders report with Bizee in 2026.

### Why does Northwest cost more than Bizee if Bizee includes a free year?

Northwest's $125/year includes Privacy by Default — using their address on your public filings to keep your home address off the record — plus same-day local document scanning and dedicated human support. Bizee's lower price reflects a more automated, tier-dependent support model and privacy that's an extra cost rather than a default feature.

### Which registered agent service offers the best privacy?

Northwest is the privacy leader among mainstream services because it uses its own address on public filings by default and does not sell customer data. Bizee offers privacy features but typically as an add-on. If keeping your personal information off public records is a top priority, Northwest is the stronger choice. [LLC Attorney](/go/llc-attorney/) also offers strong privacy for founders who want attorney-backed service.

### What happens if my registered agent misses a legal document?

If a registered agent fails to forward service of process, you could miss a lawsuit deadline and lose by default judgment, or miss a state compliance notice and have your LLC fall out of good standing. This is why reliability matters more than saving a few dollars. Both Northwest and Bizee have solid track records, but Northwest's same-day local scanning gives it an edge on reliability in my testing.

### Can I be my own registered agent instead of paying Northwest or Bizee?

Yes, in most states you can serve as your own registered agent if you have a physical street address in the state and are available during business hours. But doing so puts your address on the public record and ties you to being physically present, which is why most founders pay $119–$125/year for a commercial service. The privacy and convenience usually justify the cost.

### Is the registered agent renewal fee tax deductible?

Registered agent fees are generally a deductible ordinary and necessary business expense for an LLC, but you should confirm your specific situation with a tax professional. The IRS outlines deductible business expenses in [Publication 535](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-publication-535). This article does not constitute tax advice.
