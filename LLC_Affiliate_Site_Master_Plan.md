## 1. NICHE OVERVIEW & ECONOMICS

### Why LLC Formation

- ~4.8 million new US businesses formed annually; ~68% are LLCs (~3.3 million LLC formations/year)
- FinCEN beneficial ownership reporting (BOI) requirement creates ongoing compliance demand and fresh search volume
- Evergreen demand — people form businesses year-round with mild seasonal peaks in January and Q1
- Commissions range $60–$220 per conversion — among the highest in all affiliate marketing
- Total addressable keyword volume: 500K+ monthly searches across all LLC-related queries
- Traffic needed to hit $1,000/month: ~1,750 visitors (lowest of any niche analyzed)
- 50-state content strategy provides a built-in, systematic content engine with 500+ article targets

### Revenue Math

**Conservative scenario (Northwest Registered Agent focus):**
- 1,750 monthly visitors
- 10% CTR to affiliate links (high-intent content)
- 4% conversion rate (Northwest reports 9–25% on custom pages; 4% is conservative for organic)
- $60 commission per formation
- 1,750 × 10% × 4% × $60 = $420/month from Northwest alone

**Blended scenario (multiple programs):**
- 3,000 monthly visitors
- 8% CTR blended
- 3.5% conversion
- $100 blended commission (mix of Northwest $60, LLC Attorney $150, ZenBusiness $60–100)
- 3,000 × 8% × 3.5% × $100 = $840/month

**Target scenario ($1,000+/month):**
- ~4,000–5,000 monthly visitors with optimized content and multiple programs
- Achievable at month 8–12 with consistent publishing

## 8. SEO TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### On-Page SEO Checklist (Every Article)
- [ ] Target keyword in H1, URL, meta title, meta description
- [ ] Meta title format: "[Primary Keyword] ([Year]) | [Site Name]" — under 60 characters
- [ ] Meta description: compelling, includes keyword, under 155 characters, includes CTA
- [ ] H2s and H3s use related keywords / LSI terms naturally
- [ ] Internal links: minimum 3–5 per article to related content on site
- [ ] External links: 2–3 to authoritative sources (state.gov, IRS.gov, etc.)
- [ ] Images with descriptive alt text (include keyword where natural)
- [ ] Table of contents for articles over 2,000 words
- [ ] FAQ section with FAQ schema (JSON-LD) for 3–5 questions

### Schema Markup (JSON-LD)
- **Every page**: Organization schema, BreadcrumbList
- **Review pages**: Review schema with rating
- **Comparison pages**: Product schema for each service
- **FAQ sections**: FAQPage schema
- **How-to guides**: HowTo schema
- **State guides**: Article schema with author markup

### Technical Performance
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, FID < 100ms, CLS < 0.1
- Mobile-first design (60%+ of LLC searches are mobile)
- Proper canonical URLs
- XML sitemap with all pages
- Robots.txt allowing full crawl
- HTTPS required
- Fast hosting (Vercel/Cloudflare recommended)

### Link Building Strategy (Months 1–6)
- **Featured.com / HARO**: Respond to journalist queries about small business, entrepreneurship, LLC topics — aim for 3–5 editorial backlinks/month from DR 50+ sites
- **State-specific resource pages**: Reach out to state business development centers, SCORE chapters, local chambers of commerce for inclusion on their resource pages
- **Interactive tools**: Build LLC cost calculator and "do I need an LLC" quiz — these earn natural backlinks
- **Guest posts**: Write for small business blogs, freelancer sites, creator economy publications
- **Broken link building**: Find broken links on existing LLC/business formation resources and offer your content as replacement
- **Target**: 5–15 quality backlinks per month
